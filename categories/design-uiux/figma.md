## What it does

Figma is the dominant tool for UI/UX design — collaborative, browser-based, used at most modern tech companies. Free tier includes unlimited personal files, basic AI (Figma AI for translation/rewriting/visual search, FigJam AI for brainstorming). Pro tiers unlock advanced AI features like Figma Make (text-to-design) and Figma Buzz.

## Best use cases

- Learning UI/UX design (industry-standard tool to know)
- Personal portfolio and freelance design work
- Collaborative design with teammates
- Quick mockups and prototypes

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|

## Beginner verdict

If you're learning design, Figma is the tool to learn. Free tier covers your entire learning period. Don't pay for Pro until you have client/team work that justifies it.

- **Learning curve**: Easy (basics) / Moderate (advanced features)
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Some "free" features moved to paid in 2024 — check current limits
