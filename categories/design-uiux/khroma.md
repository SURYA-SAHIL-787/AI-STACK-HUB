## What it does

Khroma asks you to pick 50 colors you like, then trains a personal AI model to generate color palettes matching your taste. Outputs palettes with hex codes, gradients, and example images. Free with no upsell — funded by donations.

## Best use cases

- Generating brand color palettes
- Finding color combinations for UI designs
- Breaking out of "always using the same colors" rut
- Quick color inspiration for any creative project

## Beginner verdict

If you struggle with color selection, Khroma is genuinely useful — free, no signup pressure, just spend 5 minutes training it on your taste.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: 50-color preference selection (5 minutes)
- **Gotchas**: Browser-based — your training is per-browser unless you sign in
