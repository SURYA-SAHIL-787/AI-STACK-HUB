## What it does

Framer is a no-code website builder with strong design tools and AI features — generate full pages from prompts, translate sites into multiple languages, AI-powered SEO suggestions, animations. Considered the design-first alternative to Webflow (Webflow has more dev controls; Framer is more designer-friendly).

## Best use cases

- Personal portfolios and design sites
- Marketing landing pages
- Freelancer/agency client sites
- Sites where animation and visual polish matter

## Beginner verdict

If you need a personal portfolio or simple business site and don't want to learn code, Framer is the easiest path with AI assistance. Free tier publishes to framer.app subdomain — enough to evaluate.

- **Learning curve**: Easy (Mac users especially familiar with the Sketch-like feel)
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Free hosting is on framer.app; need paid tier for custom domain
