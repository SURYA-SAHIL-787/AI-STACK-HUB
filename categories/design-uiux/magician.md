## What it does

Magician adds three "wands" to Figma — Magic Text (AI copywriting), Magic Image (AI image generation), and Magic Icon (custom icons from descriptions). The simplest way to add AI to a Figma workflow without committing to Figma's Pro tier or learning multiple separate tools.

## Best use cases

- Figma users who want AI but don't want Pro pricing
- Quick placeholder copy/images while designing
- Custom icons in any style you describe
- Avoiding tool-switching during design sessions

## Beginner verdict

If you use Figma regularly, $5/mo Magician is the cheapest way to add useful AI to your workflow. Easier and cheaper than upgrading to Figma Professional just for AI.

- **Learning curve**: Easy (works inside Figma)
- **Setup required**: Install Figma plugin + Magician account
- **Gotchas**: Doesn't replace Figma's native AI — complements it
