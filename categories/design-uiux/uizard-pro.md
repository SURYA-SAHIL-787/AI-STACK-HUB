## What it does

Uizard Pro removes the 3-project / 10-template / limited-AI caps from the free tier. Adds premium templates, custom branding, and priority AI generation. Designed for users who've validated Uizard's workflow and want full access.

## Best use cases

- Frequent prototyping (multiple projects per week)
- Founders/PMs at startups using Uizard for product discussions
- Replacing Figma for users who can't justify learning it
- Bulk AI generations for design exploration

## Beginner verdict

For occasional mockups, free Uizard or alternatives are fine. Pro pays off only with consistent weekly use.

- **Learning curve**: Same as free Uizard
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from free account
- **Gotchas**: Don't buy monthly — annual is 50% cheaper
