## What it does

Galileo generates high-fidelity UI designs from text prompts — closer to "production-ready" aesthetic than Uizard's more wireframe-like outputs. Designs export to Figma for further editing. Often used as a starting point that designers then refine.

## Best use cases

- Generating polished UI starting points
- Designers wanting first drafts to iterate on
- Pitching design concepts to stakeholders
- Multiple aesthetic variations of the same flow
