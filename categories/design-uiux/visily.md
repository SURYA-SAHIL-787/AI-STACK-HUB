## What it does

Visily turns text prompts, screenshots, or hand-drawn sketches into editable UI designs. Strong on the "I have a picture of what I want" workflow — drag in any UI screenshot, get it back as editable layers. Also includes templates, components, and Figma export.

## Best use cases

- Recreating competitor app UIs as starting points
- Converting hand-drawn wireframes to digital
- Rapid PM/founder mockups
- Generating multiple variations of the same screen
