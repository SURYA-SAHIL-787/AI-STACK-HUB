## What it does

Uizard lets you describe a UI in text ("login screen with email and password, dark theme") and get an editable mockup. Also includes screenshot-to-design (paste an image of any UI, get an editable version), wireframe generation, and design themes. Built specifically for people without design backgrounds.

## Best use cases

- Founders prototyping app ideas
- PMs creating mockups for stakeholder discussions
- Quick UI concepts without learning Figma
- Converting competitor screenshots into editable designs

## Beginner verdict

If you're not a designer but need mockups occasionally, Uizard is faster than learning Figma. For serious design work, Figma is still the answer.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: AI-generated designs feel templated; refine manually for production use
