## What it does

Charisma.ai is a no-code platform for designing branching, AI-driven narrative — characters with memory and emotion react to player input. Used in BBC interactive content, indie games, and training simulations.

## Best use cases

- Narrative indie games with deep dialogue trees
- Training scenarios with branching outcomes
- Educational interactive content
- Multiplayer chat-driven games

| Tier | Price | What you get |

## Beginner verdict

Strong choice for narrative-heavy games. Less code than Inworld; less voice than Convai.

## Alternatives

- [Inworld](inworld.md) — broader NPC platform
