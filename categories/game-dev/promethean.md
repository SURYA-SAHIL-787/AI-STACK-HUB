## What it does

Promethean AI helps build 3D environments by placing assets where they make sense — you describe a "messy teenager's bedroom," it lays out the props. Used by major studios for level design and previz.

## Best use cases

- AAA studio level design / set dressing
- Previz for film and games
- Procedural environment generation
- Cutting weeks of asset placement
