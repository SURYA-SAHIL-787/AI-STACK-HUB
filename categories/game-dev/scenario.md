## What it does

Scenario lets you train a custom model on your game's existing art, then generate new assets in that same style. Solves the "generic AI art doesn't match my game" problem.

## Best use cases

- Generating consistent props, characters, environments
- Scaling art production for indie studios
- Iterating on art directions fast
- Building style libraries for game series

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free Trial | $0 | Limited training and generations |
| Creator | $15/mo | Personal use, more credits |
| Pro | $50/mo | Higher limits, commercial license |

## Beginner verdict

Best AI art tool for game devs. Generic SD/Midjourney don't match this for style consistency.

## Alternatives

- Midjourney — generic but high quality
- [Sloyd](../3d-animation/sloyd.md) — for 3D not 2D
