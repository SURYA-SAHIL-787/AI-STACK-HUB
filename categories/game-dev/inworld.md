## What it does

Inworld lets you design AI characters with personality, backstory, voice, and memory — then drop them into Unity, Unreal, or Roblox. Used in mods (notably for Skyrim) to give NPCs real conversation.

## Best use cases

- Indie RPGs with talking NPCs
- Skyrim/Fallout-style mods adding AI dialogue
- Educational games with mentor characters
- Customer-facing brand mascots
