## What it does

AI Dungeon is an AI-driven text adventure where the story responds to your input. For game devs, it's a sandbox to prototype branching narratives, test what story patterns hold up, and explore mechanics before building.

## Best use cases

- Prototyping interactive fiction structure
- Testing whether your story premise sustains play
- Generating quest hooks for your game
- Inspiration for procedurally-generated story games
