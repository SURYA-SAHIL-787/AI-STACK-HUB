## What it does

Convai is an AI NPC platform competing with Inworld. Strong on Unreal Engine integration, voice + lip sync, and longer character memory. Used in MetaHuman demos and indie Unreal projects.

## Best use cases

- Unreal Engine AI NPC implementation
- MetaHuman characters with conversation
- VR/AR experiences with talking characters
- Customer service avatars built on Unreal

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Indie | $20/mo | Up to 100 hours of dialogue/mo |
| Pro | $80/mo | More hours, more characters |
| Enterprise | Custom | SLAs, custom integration |

## Beginner verdict

Best paid pick if you're on Unreal. Inworld is the alternative if on Unity.
