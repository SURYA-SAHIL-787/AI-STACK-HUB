## What it does

Consensus answers questions like "Does intermittent fasting work?" or "Is caffeine bad for kids?" with summaries of what scientific papers actually say — including a "Consensus Meter" showing agreement levels across studies. Built on 200M+ papers. Also available as a Custom GPT in ChatGPT.

## Best use cases

- Quick fact-checking before writing
- Settling debates with actual research
- Health/nutrition/science questions
- Journalism and content creation

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Unlimited searches, limited AI features |
| Premium | $9/mo (annual) or $11.99/mo | Full AI Consensus Meter, GPT-4 summaries |
| Enterprise | Custom | Team features |

## Beginner verdict

Cheapest serious research AI ($9/mo Premium). Free tier is also genuinely useful for casual fact-checking.
