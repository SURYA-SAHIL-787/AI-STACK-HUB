## What it does

Litmaps combines Connected-Papers-style visualization with ongoing monitoring — set up a "literature map" around your topic and get notified when new related papers appear. Strong for staying current in fast-moving research areas. AI-suggested expansion finds papers you might have missed.

## Best use cases

- PhD students staying current on their thesis topic
- Researchers monitoring active fields (AI, ML, biotech)
- Setting up literature alerts for grant proposals
- Maintaining ongoing reading lists

## Beginner verdict

Specifically useful if you need to track ongoing research in a field, not just one-time exploration. For one-off use, Connected Papers (cheaper at $3/mo) suffices.

## Alternatives

- [Connected Papers](connected-papers.md) — cheaper one-shot exploration
- [Semantic Scholar](semantic-scholar.md) — free baseline
- ResearchRabbit (free, similar feature set)
