## What it does

Elicit searches across 125M+ academic papers, finds the most relevant ones for your question, and uses AI to extract structured data — methods, results, sample size, findings — into a comparison table. Can summarize evidence across many papers in seconds. Free tier provides limited monthly credits.

## Best use cases

- Writing literature reviews for theses/papers
- Quick "what does research say about X?" exploration
- Comparing methodologies across studies
- Generating starting bibliographies

## Beginner verdict

Best free starting point for AI-assisted academic research. Free tier covers a typical literature review session.
