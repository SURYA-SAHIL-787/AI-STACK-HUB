## What it does

Semantic Scholar is Allen Institute for AI's free academic search engine — 220M+ papers indexed with AI-generated TLDRs (one-sentence summaries), influence scores, and citation context. The underlying data powers many other research tools (Connected Papers, Elicit). The API is free for academic use.

## Best use cases

- Free academic search with AI summaries
- Building research tools (the free API is generous)
- Finding influential papers in any field
- Replacing Google Scholar for AI-augmented search

## Beginner verdict

Should be every student's bookmark — free, no signup, AI summaries on every paper. The Allen AI nonprofit funding makes this sustainable.

## Alternatives

- Google Scholar (free, no AI summaries)
- [Connected Papers](connected-papers.md) — visual exploration
- [Elicit](elicit.md) — for synthesis
