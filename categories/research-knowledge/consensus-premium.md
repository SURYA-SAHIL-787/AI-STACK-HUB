## What it does

Consensus Premium unlocks unlimited AI-powered Consensus Meter analyses (the "does the science agree?" feature), GPT-4 paper summaries, study quality indicators, and advanced search filters. Annual pricing at $9/mo is among the cheapest paid research AI tiers.

## Best use cases

- Daily science writers and journalists
- Evidence-based bloggers and YouTubers
- Policy analysts citing research
- Healthcare professionals validating claims

| Tier | Price | What you get |

## Beginner verdict

Cheapest serious research AI subscription. If fact-checking against papers is part of your work, $9/mo is fair.
