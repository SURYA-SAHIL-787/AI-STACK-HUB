## What it does

Elicit Plus removes the free-tier credit cap (5k → 20k/mo), unlocks advanced data extraction (custom columns from papers, comparison tables), and provides higher search result limits. Best value tier for individual researchers.

## Best use cases

- Master's/PhD students doing regular literature work
- Researchers writing review papers
- Academic consultants extracting data across many studies
- Replacing manual paper screening workflows

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Plus | $12/mo (annual) | 20,000 credits, custom extractions |
| Pro | $49/mo | 60,000 credits, all features |
| Teams | $99/user/mo | Workspace, collaboration |

## Beginner verdict

If you're using Elicit Free regularly and hitting the cap, $12/mo annual is the no-brainer upgrade.
