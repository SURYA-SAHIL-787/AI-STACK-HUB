## What it does

Paste one seminal paper, get a beautiful visualization showing the most-related papers in the field — clustered by similarity. Helps you discover both predecessors and offshoots of any work. Doesn't use just citations — uses Semantic Scholar's co-citation graph for semantic similarity.

## Best use cases

- Starting a literature review from one good paper
- Finding influential related work you might have missed
- Mapping out a research area visually
- Identifying paper "neighborhoods" in unfamiliar fields

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 5 graphs per month |

## Beginner verdict

Free tier covers occasional use; Academic at $3/mo is the cheapest research AI subscription anywhere — no reason not to upgrade if you're researching daily.
