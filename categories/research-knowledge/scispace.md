## What it does

SciSpace's Copilot is built to make papers readable — upload a PDF or paste a paper, then ask "what's the main finding?", "explain Figure 3", or "what methodology did they use?" Built for the experience of "I have to read 5 papers tonight and don't understand half the jargon."

## Best use cases

- Reading papers outside your expertise
- Quick paper summaries before deep reads
- Explaining specific figures, tables, equations
- Multi-paper Q&A sessions

## Beginner verdict

Best tool for actually reading papers, not just searching them. Pair with Elicit for full research workflow.
