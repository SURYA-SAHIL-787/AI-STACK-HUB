---

## What it does

SciSpace Premium removes the free tier's daily question limit, gives unlimited Copilot conversations, premium AI models (GPT-4, Claude), and advanced literature review features (matrix analysis across many papers).

## Best use cases

- PhD students with heavy reading lists
- Postdocs doing systematic reviews
- Industry researchers needing to understand academic work fast
- Faculty managing reading for courses they teach

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Premium | $20/mo | Unlimited Copilot, premium models |
| Premium Plus | $40/mo | More credits, advanced search, priority |

## Beginner verdict

Higher price than Elicit Plus / Consensus Premium. Justified specifically for users who spend hours reading papers daily.

- [Elicit Plus](elicit-plus.md) — cheaper, synthesis-focused
- [Consensus Premium](consensus-premium.md) — cheaper, question-focused
