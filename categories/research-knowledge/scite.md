## What it does

Scite is the only major tool that classifies citations by sentiment — when 47 papers cite a study, Scite tells you how many SUPPORT it, how many CONTRAST it, how many just MENTION it. Reveals if a famous result has been replicated or contradicted. Includes an AI Assistant for research questions backed by evidence with classified citations.

## Best use cases

- Verifying that a claim hasn't been contradicted by newer research
- Writing evidence-based papers where citation quality matters
- Building credibility (you cite papers that have actually been supported)
- Researchers in fast-moving fields where replication matters
