## What it does

DoNotPay markets itself as "the world's first robot lawyer" — AI that helps consumers with disputes, refund requests, small claims, parking tickets, and canceling subscriptions. Aimed at non-lawyers fighting common consumer issues.

## Best use cases

- Disputing a parking ticket
- Requesting refunds from companies
- Canceling subscriptions that won't cancel
- Drafting small claims court letters

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free chat | $0 | Limited features |
| Subscription | $36/3 months | Full features |

## Beginner verdict

Best for consumers. Skip if you're a lawyer or running business legal work.
