## What it does

Lexis+ AI brings AI on top of the LexisNexis legal database — generates research memos, drafts clauses, and answers natural-language legal questions with citations to actual case law. Built into LexisNexis users' existing workflows.

## Best use cases

- AI research with verified case law citations
- Drafting memos with research backing
- Existing LexisNexis users adding AI without switching
- Multi-jurisdiction legal questions

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | Custom | Full platform + Lexis database |

Quote-based. Most firms already pay for LexisNexis; this is the AI add-on.

## Beginner verdict

The right pick if your firm is already on LexisNexis. Skip if you're on Westlaw.
