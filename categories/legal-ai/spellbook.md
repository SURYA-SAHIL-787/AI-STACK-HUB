## What it does

Spellbook is a Microsoft Word add-in that reviews contracts — suggests redlines, flags missing clauses, explains risky language. Strong fit for transactional lawyers doing M&A, commercial, employment contracts.

## Best use cases

- Solo lawyer reviewing inbound contracts faster
- Small firm boutiques without a paralegal team
- First-pass markup before partner review
- Flagging common issues in NDAs and MSAs
