## What it does

Robin AI reviews contracts, answers questions about them in plain English, and supports redlining. Higher up the market than Spellbook — used by mid-market in-house counsel and law firms.

## Best use cases

- In-house counsel doing contract triage
- Q&A over large contract libraries
- Faster review of inbound vendor agreements
