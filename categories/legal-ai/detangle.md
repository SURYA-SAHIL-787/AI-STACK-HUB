## What it does

Upload a contract, NDA, lease, or terms of service — Detangle summarizes it in plain English, flags red flags, and explains key clauses. Built for consumers, not lawyers.

## Best use cases

- Understanding an apartment lease before signing
- Reviewing an employment offer or NDA
- Reading Terms of Service before clicking accept
- Understanding what a settlement actually means

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |

## Beginner verdict

Use this before signing anything important. Free tier handles most one-off needs.
