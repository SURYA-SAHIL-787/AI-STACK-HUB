## What it does

Not a legal tool, but heavily used by lawyers and individuals to draft contract first passes, explain legal jargon, summarize cases, and brainstorm arguments. Important: not a substitute for a lawyer; outputs need review.

## Best use cases

- First-draft NDAs, employment letters, simple contracts
- Plain-English explanations of legal documents
- Brainstorming arguments before structured research
- Summarizing court opinions or statutes

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |

## Beginner verdict

Useful starting point — but never the final document. Always have a lawyer review.
