## What it does

Ironclad is a CLM platform — manages contracts from drafting through signature, repository, and renewal. AI features include redlining, clause extraction, risk scoring, and natural-language Q&A across contract libraries.

## Best use cases

- In-house legal scaling beyond email + PDFs
- Centralized contract repository with AI search
- Auto-drafting from clause libraries
- Tracking obligations and renewals across thousands of contracts
