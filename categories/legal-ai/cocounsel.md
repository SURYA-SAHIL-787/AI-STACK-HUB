## What it does

CoCounsel (originally Casetext, now Thomson Reuters) is an AI legal assistant tied to Westlaw. Handles research, document review, deposition prep, and contract analysis. Strong on US case law.

## Best use cases

- Existing Westlaw firms adding AI
- Deposition and discovery preparation
- Document review at scale (litigation)
- Contract analysis with case law context

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | Custom | Full platform + Westlaw integration |

Quote-based. Most law firms already pay for Westlaw; this layers on.

## Beginner verdict

Right pick if you're on Westlaw. Skip if you're on LexisNexis.
