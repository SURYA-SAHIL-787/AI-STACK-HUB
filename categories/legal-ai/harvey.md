## What it does

Harvey is the AI of choice at top law firms (Allen & Overy, PwC Legal, etc.). It handles drafting, research, due diligence, and matter management. Custom-trained on each firm's documents.

## Best use cases

- AmLaw 100 firms deploying AI across practice groups
- Large in-house teams unifying legal AI
- Custom-trained AI on a firm's precedents
- Cross-practice research (litigation, M&A, regulatory)

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom training + dedicated CSM |

Six- to seven-figure annual contracts. Not for solo lawyers.

## Beginner verdict

Not for individuals or small firms. The right pick when you're a large firm.
