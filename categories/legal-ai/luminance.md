## What it does

Luminance is a UK-founded AI platform for contract review and document analysis. Especially strong in M&A due diligence — surfacing anomalies, clustering similar clauses, flagging risk in large doc sets.

## Best use cases

- M&A due diligence across thousands of docs
- Cross-border deals (multi-language support)
- Litigation discovery doc review
- Large-scale contract audits

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | Custom | Full platform + onboarding |

Quote-based; typically five- to six-figure annual.

## Beginner verdict

Niche but strong for M&A-heavy firms. Skip for general practice.
