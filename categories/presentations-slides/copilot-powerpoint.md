## What it does

Copilot for PowerPoint generates decks from prompts ("Make a 10-slide deck about Q4 marketing strategy"), transforms Word documents into presentations, summarizes existing decks, and rewrites slides for tone/clarity. Tightly integrated with the rest of Microsoft 365.

## Best use cases

- PowerPoint users in enterprises
- Converting Word docs to presentations
- Updating old decks with AI rewrites
- Replacing standalone AI deck tools when M365 Copilot is already paid for

## Beginner verdict

Only worth it if you're committed to PowerPoint AND your employer covers M365 Copilot. Standalone, Gamma at $10 produces better outputs.

- **Learning curve**: Easy (works inside PowerPoint)
- **Setup required**: M365 Copilot subscription
- **Gotchas**: Output sometimes feels generic — manually edit for high-stakes decks
