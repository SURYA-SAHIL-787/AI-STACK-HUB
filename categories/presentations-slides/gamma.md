## What it does

Gamma generates presentations, documents, and webpages from a single prompt — or by importing existing content (PDFs, Word docs, URLs). Picks a template, generates outline, fills in slides with text + AI images, all in under a minute. Most viral AI presentation tool of 2024–2025.

## Best use cases

- Students making project presentations the night before
- Founders pitching ideas to investors/co-founders
- Quick internal updates and team presentations
- Converting blog posts/docs into slide decks
