## What it does

Gamma Plus is the natural step up from free Gamma — 400 credits/month (refreshed), no Gamma branding on exports, custom font upload, and access to the latest AI models. The cheapest tier that produces "professional" outputs.

## Best use cases

- Regular deck producers (1+ per week)
- Client-facing presentations (no branding required)
- Personal/professional projects that need polish
- Replacing Beautiful.ai/Tome at lower cost

If you make decks more than monthly, $10 Plus is worth it. For 1-2 decks/year, free tier covers you.

- **Learning curve**: Same as free Gamma
