## What it does

Decktopus Pro unlocks unlimited decks, full brand customization (upload logos, fonts, colors), all exports (PDF, PPTX, online sharing), and multilingual features. Aimed at sales/marketing teams that need consistent branding across many decks.

## Best use cases

- Sales reps producing many client-tailored decks
- Marketing teams enforcing brand standards
- Translating decks for international audiences
- Replacing manual brand-application work

## Beginner verdict

For most users, Gamma Plus at $10 covers similar needs more cheaply. Decktopus Pro pays off specifically for brand-heavy use cases.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from free Decktopus
- **Gotchas**: 3-deck free limit makes evaluation feel cramped
