## What it does

Tome differentiates by treating presentations as scrolling narratives — slides flow with smooth transitions, less "card stack" and more "interactive story." AI generates outline, copy, and visuals from prompts. Pivoted in 2024 toward AI-native B2B sales workflows.

## Best use cases

- B2B sales decks and outbound pitches
- Narrative pitch decks (founders pitching VCs)
- Marketing landing-page-style presentations
- Conference talks with strong story flow

## Beginner verdict

If your decks are story-driven (pitch, sales, narrative), Tome's flow is unique. For traditional decks, Gamma is more universal.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Tome's pivot to B2B sales focus may limit consumer features over time
