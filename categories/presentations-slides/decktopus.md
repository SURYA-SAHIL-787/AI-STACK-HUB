## What it does

Decktopus generates decks like Gamma but with more emphasis on brand consistency — you input brand colors, fonts, and tone, and outputs respect those. Good for marketers and sales reps who need to keep visual consistency across many decks.

## Best use cases

- Marketing teams creating campaign-specific decks
- Sales reps tailoring pitches to prospects
- Brand-sensitive presentations
- Multilingual decks (auto-translation built in)

## Beginner verdict

Try Gamma first. Switch to Decktopus only if brand consistency is critical and you have brand assets to plug in.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: 3-deck free limit hits fast
