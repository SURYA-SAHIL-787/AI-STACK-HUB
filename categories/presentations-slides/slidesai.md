## What it does

SlidesAI is a Google Workspace add-on that generates slides from text inputs without leaving Google Slides. Paste an outline or topic, pick a template, get slides. Free tier covers ~12 presentations/month.

## Best use cases

- Students who use Google Workspace for school
- Anyone already invested in Google Slides
- Quick slide generation without learning new tools
- Adding AI to existing Google Slides workflow

## Pricing breakdown

## Beginner verdict

If your school/work mandates Google Slides, SlidesAI saves time. For standalone projects, Gamma is more powerful.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Install Google Workspace add-on
- **Gotchas**: Slide quality is templated — needs manual polish
