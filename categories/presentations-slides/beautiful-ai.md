## What it does

Beautiful.ai uses "smart slide" templates that auto-adjust as you add content — drop in 3 bullets, slide rebalances; add a 4th, it adjusts again. AI features include generating slides from prompts, recommending visuals, and ensuring brand consistency. Predates the Gamma AI trend by years.

## Best use cases

- Investor pitch decks (clean, professional look)
- Executive presentations where polish matters
- Marketing teams needing on-brand consistency
- Replacing manual design work for serious decks

## Beginner verdict

If you're pitching investors or presenting to executives, Beautiful.ai's polish is worth $12. For casual decks, Gamma is enough.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Paid account from start (limited free trial)
- **Gotchas**: Smart slide templates can feel restrictive vs free-form design
