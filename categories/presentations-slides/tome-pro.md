## What it does

Tome Pro removes free-tier AI limits and unlocks templates plus their newer B2B sales-focused workflows (account targeting, prospect-specific decks). Tome's 2024 pivot focused them on enterprise sales rather than general use.

## Best use cases

- B2B sales reps with target accounts
- Founders pitching investors with narrative flow
- Marketing teams creating story-driven content
- Replacing manual sales-deck personalization
