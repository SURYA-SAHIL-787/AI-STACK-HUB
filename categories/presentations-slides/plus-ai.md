## What it does

Plus AI generates slides inside Google Slides from prompts, outlines, or even URLs. Differentiator: edits existing slides (rewrite, restyle, add/remove sections), exports to PowerPoint with full fidelity, and has stronger templates than competitors. YC-backed with strong user growth.

## Best use cases

- Google Slides users wanting AI without switching tools
- Editing existing decks (not just creating new ones)
- Teams needing PowerPoint export from Google Slides
- Updating old decks with AI rewrite

## Beginner verdict

For Google Slides users, Plus AI is more powerful than SlidesAI. For students with no preference, Gamma's standalone tool is easier.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Install Google Workspace add-on
- **Gotchas**: Best features need Pro tier — free is limited
