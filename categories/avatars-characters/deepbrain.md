## What it does

DeepBrain (AI Studios) generates AI human videos with broadcast-quality avatars. Especially strong on Korean, Japanese, and Chinese voice + lip sync. Used by Korean TV networks and major brands in APAC.

## Best use cases

- East-Asian language content with native-quality lip sync
- Broadcast-quality news/explainer videos
- Branded virtual hosts
- Custom AI human cloning (paid tiers)
