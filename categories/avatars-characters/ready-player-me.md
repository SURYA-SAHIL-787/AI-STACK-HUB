## What it does

Free avatar creator — take a selfie or design from scratch, get a 3D avatar usable across 3000+ apps including VRChat, Spatial, and other metaverse platforms. Devs integrate via SDK.

## Best use cases

- One avatar for all your VR/metaverse accounts
- Game devs adding avatar support without building one
- Virtual events with personal avatars
- Profile pictures with 3D depth
