## What it does

Hour One generates AI presenter videos from text scripts. Catalog of stock presenters, custom presenter option, brand templates. Focused on corporate L&D — less marketing flash than HeyGen.

## Best use cases

- Internal L&D training modules
- Onboarding videos at scale
- Quick how-to videos from written SOPs
- Course content for online academies

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Lite | $30/mo | 10 mins/mo |
| Business | $115/mo | 25 mins, more presenters |
| Enterprise | Custom | SSO, custom presenters |

## Beginner verdict

Solid alternative to Synthesia for L&D teams. Pricing is competitive.
