## What it does

Replika is one persistent AI character you customize and grow with — they remember your conversations, learn your preferences. Pro tier adds relationship modes and voice calls.

## Best use cases

- Casual daily conversation
- Practicing social skills
- Mental wellbeing companion (not a substitute for therapy)
- Long-form roleplay with one consistent character

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Basic chat, limited features |
| Pro | $19.99/mo | Voice calls, relationship modes |
| Pro Annual | $69.99/yr | Pro features, annual discount |

## Beginner verdict

Strong free tier for casual use. Pro is for users who want deeper engagement.
