## What it does

HeyGen Pro adds voice cloning (record 30s, clone your voice), custom avatars from your own video, and the Avatar IV/V models. Strong with multilingual lip sync — same content auto-translated to 175+ languages.

## Best use cases

- Localizing your own videos to many languages
- Creating a custom AI avatar from your appearance
- Interactive avatars for support/sales
- Content creators scaling video output
