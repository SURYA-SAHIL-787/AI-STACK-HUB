## What it does

HeyGen's free tier gives you 1 minute of avatar video per month — enough to test the quality. Choose an avatar, type a script, get a video with realistic lip sync in 100+ languages.

## Best use cases

- One-off demo videos
- Comparing HeyGen vs Synthesia before buying
- Trying voice cloning (paid tier)
