## What it does

Synthesia generates AI presenter videos from text scripts. 230+ avatars in 140+ languages, with brand-consistent templates. Used by Fortune 500s for training videos at scale.

## Best use cases

- Corporate training videos (compliance, onboarding)
- Localizing one script into many languages
- Internal comms videos at scale
- Marketing/explainer videos

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
