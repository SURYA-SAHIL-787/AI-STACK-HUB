## What it does

D-ID animates still images into talking-head videos with realistic lip sync. Upload a photo, type a script, get a video. Used heavily for marketing, e-learning, and social content.

## Best use cases

- Animating historical figures for educational content
- Quick marketing videos from a portrait
- Multilingual versions of the same video
- Embedding in apps via API
