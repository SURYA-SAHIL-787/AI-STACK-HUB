## What it does

Colossyan generates AI avatar videos with strong support for multi-avatar dialogue scenes — useful for training scenarios where two characters interact (manager + employee, doctor + patient). 70+ languages.

## Best use cases

- Compliance training with scenario-based scripts
- Multi-character explainer videos
- Multilingual L&D content
- Sales role-play videos

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $35/mo | 10 mins/mo, basic avatars |
| Pro | $90/mo | 25 mins, more avatars |

Strong choice when you need two avatars talking to each other.
