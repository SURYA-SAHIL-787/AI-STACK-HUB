## What it does

Character.AI is a platform of millions of AI character personas built by the community. Chat with Einstein, Shakespeare, anime characters, study buddies. Free tier has waitlists at peak; Plus removes them.

## Best use cases

- Language practice with conversational AI
- Roleplay and creative writing prompts
- Casual chat with personas
- Studying with persona-based tutors

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Unlimited chats, may queue at peak |
| Plus (C.AI+) | $9.99/mo | No wait time, faster responses |

## Beginner verdict

The most popular AI character chat platform. Free tier is plenty.
