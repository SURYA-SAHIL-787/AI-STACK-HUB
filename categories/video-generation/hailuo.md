## What it does

Hailuo AI (from Chinese AI company MiniMax) offers free AI video generation that punched above its weight when released. The video model is competitive with paid offerings, and the free tier has been unusually generous — no credit card, fast generation, decent quality.

## Best use cases

- First-time AI video experimentation
- Free alternative to Runway/Pika after credits expire
- Quick concept tests without sign-up friction
- Image-to-video animation

Easiest no-friction free AI video. Avoid for sensitive content; great for casual experimentation.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
