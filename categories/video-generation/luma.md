## What it does

Luma Dream Machine generates photorealistic video clips from text or image prompts. Strong on natural motion, realistic physics, and cinematic camera moves. Free tier provides ~30 generations per month — among the most generous in the category.

## Best use cases

- Photorealistic short clips for content
- Animating existing photos into video
- Establishing shots, b-roll, mood pieces
- Experimentation without paying

## Beginner verdict

If your goal is realistic-looking video, start here — the free tier alone produces impressive results. Pair with Runway for editing.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Free tier resets monthly; queue can be slow during peak times
