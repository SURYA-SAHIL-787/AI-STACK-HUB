## What it does

Kling was a viral hit when released in 2024 — generating 2-minute videos with strong physical realism (water, hair, fabric movement). Free tier offers daily credits. Supports text-to-video, image-to-video, and "Elements" (multiple inputs combined).

## Best use cases

- Longer video clips (up to 2 minutes)
- Scenes with complex physics (movement, interactions)
- Free alternative when Runway/Luma credits run out
- Image-to-video animation

## Beginner verdict

Useful free fallback when other tools' free credits run out. Avoid for sensitive/proprietary work due to data jurisdiction.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Data sent to Chinese servers — check before using for sensitive content
