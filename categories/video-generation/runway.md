## What it does

Runway is the most-used AI video platform. It combines a full browser-based video editor with their proprietary Gen-3/Gen-4 video models. Beyond text-to-video, it includes Motion Brush, lip sync, frame interpolation, green screen removal, video-to-video transformation, and Act-One (turn yourself acting into AI characters).

## Best use cases

- Short-form content for social media
- Cinematic / film-style sequences
- Animating still images into video
- Replacing green screen / VFX workflows

## Beginner verdict

The natural first AI video tool to try. 525 free credits gives ~30–50 generations to evaluate, then upgrade only if you'll keep using it.

- **Learning curve**: Easy (UI is approachable)
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Free credits don't refresh — once gone, you need to upgrade or wait for promos
