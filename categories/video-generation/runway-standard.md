## What it does

Runway Standard gives you 625 credits per month (~30–60 generations), removes the watermark, unlocks 4K video upscaling, faster generation, and the full editor including Motion Brush, Act-One, lip sync, and green screen removal.

## Best use cases

- Producing 1–2 finished AI videos per week
- Social-media-grade content creators
- Replacing some stock footage purchases
- Mid-level video freelance work

## Beginner verdict

If you've used the free Runway credits and want to keep going, $15 is reasonable. For occasional use, alternate between Luma free + Pika free instead.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from free Runway
- **Gotchas**: Credits don't roll over — use 'em or lose 'em
