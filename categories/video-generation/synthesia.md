## What it does

Synthesia produces "talking head" presenter videos using AI avatars. You write a script, pick from 230+ avatars (or create a custom AI clone of yourself), choose a language (140+ supported), and get a finished video with the avatar speaking your script naturally. Heavy enterprise adoption for training and internal comms.

## Best use cases

- Corporate training and onboarding videos
- Product explainer videos at scale
- Multilingual content (translate one script into 140 languages)
- Internal company announcements where filming a real person is impractical

## Beginner verdict

Specialized tool — only buy if you need talking-head videos at scale. For general video gen, Runway/Luma are better picks.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Paid account from day one
- **Gotchas**: No free tier (short demos only); commit before subscribing

## Alternatives

- HeyGen (not in this list) — direct competitor
- D-ID (not in this list) — avatar animation alternative
