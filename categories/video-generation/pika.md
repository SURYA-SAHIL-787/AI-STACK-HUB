## What it does

Pika focuses on accessible AI video — text-to-video, image-to-video, and signature "Pikaffects" (explode, crush, melt, deflate, squish on any object in a video). Recent versions (2.1, 2.2) added higher quality and longer clips. The Pika Twists / Scenes features animate uploaded images.

## Best use cases

- Social media content (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)
- Quick visual effects on existing footage
- Animating photographs into short videos
- Memes and creative video experiments

## Ratings from around the web

## Beginner verdict

Most fun first AI video tool — the Pikaffects are immediately rewarding. For "serious" video work, Runway or Veo are better.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Free watermark on exports; clips can be short

## Alternatives

- [Runway](runway.md) — more powerful, fewer free credits long-term
- [Luma](luma.md) — better photorealism
- [Kling](kling.md) — better at physics
