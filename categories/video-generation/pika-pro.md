## What it does

Pika Pro removes free-tier limits — no watermark, more credits (2,300/mo), faster generation queue, and access to the latest model versions. The Pikaffects (explode, crush, melt, etc.) work on any video, making it a unique offering vs Runway/Luma.

## Best use cases

- Daily social media content creation (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)
- Creative video effects on existing footage
- High-volume short-form video producers
- Replacing Adobe After Effects for simple effects

## Beginner verdict

For creative effects, Pika is unique. For pure text-to-video quality, Runway/Luma are stronger at similar prices.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from free Pika
- **Gotchas**: Standard tier ($10) is enough for casual use; Pro mainly for daily creators
