## What it does

Sora is OpenAI's text-to-video model, available via the dedicated Sora app or inside ChatGPT for paid subscribers. Strong on prompt comprehension and integrated with the OpenAI ecosystem. ChatGPT Plus users get a monthly allowance; Pro users get more.

## Best use cases

- ChatGPT Plus subscribers wanting included video gen
- Conversational iteration (refine via chat)
- Marketing teams already invested in OpenAI ecosystem
- Quick prototypes alongside ChatGPT work

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |

## Beginner verdict

If you already pay for ChatGPT Plus, Sora is free to use up to your allowance. Otherwise, Runway/Luma/Veo are more directly purchasable.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: ChatGPT Plus account
- **Gotchas**: Strict safety filters; some prompts get blocked

## Alternatives

- [Runway Standard](runway-standard.md) — direct subscription
