## What it does

Veo 3 is Google's flagship AI video model. Outputs are considered state-of-the-art — strong cinematography, complex scenes, native audio synthesis (Veo 3 can generate matching sound), and multi-shot sequences. Accessed via Google AI Ultra subscription or limited use via Gemini Advanced.

## Best use cases

- Best-possible AI video quality
- Filmmakers and agencies producing client work
- Marketing videos with cinematic feel
- Anyone for whom quality is the top priority

## Beginner verdict

Not a casual-user tool. Justified only if you produce video professionally and quality justifies $250/mo. For most users, Runway or Luma is far better value.

- **Learning curve**: Easy (the model is good — the price is the gate)
- **Setup required**: Google AI Ultra subscription
- **Gotchas**: Pricing tier is expensive; quality may not justify cost for non-professionals

## Alternatives

- [Sora](sora.md) — cheaper via ChatGPT Plus, similar tier quality
- [Runway Pro](runway.md) — broader editor, much lower price
- [Luma](luma.md) — best free/low-cost alternative
