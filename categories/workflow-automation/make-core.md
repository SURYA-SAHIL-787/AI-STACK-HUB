## What it does

Make Core gives you 10,000 operations/month at $10.59/mo — significantly cheaper than Zapier Pro per operation. Unlocks 1-minute trigger intervals (vs 2-min on free), more concurrent scenarios, and removes the limit on advanced features. Standard tier for serious Make users.

## Best use cases

- Replacing Zapier Pro to save money
- Complex workflows with iteration and branching
- Time-sensitive automations needing 1-minute intervals
- Multi-channel content distribution
