## What it does

Zapier connects 6,000+ apps (Gmail, Slack, Google Sheets, Notion, Salesforce, etc.) to automate workflows. Create "Zaps" that trigger on events ("new email arrives") and perform actions ("add row to spreadsheet"). Now includes AI features — generate Zaps from natural language, AI-powered actions inside workflows.

## Best use cases

- Auto-saving email attachments to Drive/Dropbox
- Cross-posting content between social platforms
- Lead capture: form submission → CRM → Slack notification
- Recurring reports: scheduled trigger → data sources → email digest

## Beginner verdict

The easiest first automation tool. Free 100 tasks/mo is enough to evaluate. Upgrade when you actually need it.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Free tier limits to 2-step Zaps only — multi-step needs Pro
