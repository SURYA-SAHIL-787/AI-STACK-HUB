## What it does

Bardeen automates things you do in the browser — extract data from any website, auto-fill forms, send personalized LinkedIn messages, scrape lists into spreadsheets, trigger workflows from any web page. AI agents can take multi-step actions across sites. Stronger on browser tasks than Zapier/Make.

## Best use cases

- Sales reps doing LinkedIn outreach
- Recruiters processing candidate lists
- Researchers scraping data from websites
- Customer-support automating ticket follow-ups

## Beginner verdict

If your work involves repetitive browser tasks, Bardeen is uniquely useful. For non-browser automation, Zapier/Make are better.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Chrome extension + free account
- **Gotchas**: LinkedIn automation may risk account warnings — use carefully
