## What it does

Lindy lets you create AI agents ("Lindies") that perform multi-step tasks — schedule meetings via email, qualify leads from inbox, take meeting notes, send follow-ups, manage calendar. Goes beyond static "if-this-then-that" automation by adapting to context like a junior assistant would.

## Best use cases

- Email + calendar management ("schedule a 30-min with X next week")
- Lead qualification from inbound emails
- Meeting note-taker + follow-up sender
- Sales reps automating CRM updates

## Beginner verdict

Pricier than traditional automation, but unique capability for AI-agent workflows. Try the free trial — if it saves you 5+ hours/month on email/scheduling, it pays for itself.

- **Learning curve**: Moderate (agent prompting takes practice)
- **Setup required**: Paid account, connect tools
- **Gotchas**: Credit-based pricing — heavy email use can consume fast
