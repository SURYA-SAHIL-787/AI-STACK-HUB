## What it does

IFTTT is the consumer-friendly automation tool. Pick a trigger ("when I post on Instagram") and an action ("save photo to Dropbox") — done. Hugely popular for smart home automation (connect Philips Hue, Alexa, Google Home), social media cross-posting, and personal life hacks.

## Best use cases

- Smart home automation
- Social media cross-posting (Instagram → Twitter, etc.)
- Personal life automations (weather alerts, RSS-to-email)
- Connecting consumer apps

## Beginner verdict

Best for personal/consumer use cases. For business workflows, Zapier/Make are stronger. Pro+ tier added AI features but $14.99 puts it close to Zapier territory.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: 2-applet free tier is restrictive — Pro at $3.49 is the practical starting point

## Alternatives

- [Zapier](zapier.md) — business-focused
- [Make](make.md) — better value, visual
- Apple Shortcuts (not in this list) — for iOS users
