## What it does

Zapier Professional removes the 2-step Zap limit (lets you build multi-step workflows), unlocks premium apps (Stripe, Salesforce, Quickbooks), gives 750 tasks/month, and adds advanced features like paths (if/else logic), formatters, and webhooks.

## Best use cases

- Multi-step workflows beyond free tier's limit
- Connecting premium business apps
- Daily automation for small businesses
- Replacing manual data entry across tools

## Beginner verdict

If you've hit free Zapier limits, Pro is the natural step. For tighter budgets, Make Core at $10.59 does similar work cheaper.

- **Learning curve**: Same as free Zapier
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from free Zapier
- **Gotchas**: Annual billing is 33% cheaper than monthly
