## What it does

Claude is Anthropic's conversational AI. The free tier gives you Claude Sonnet 4.6 (Anthropic's mid-tier frontier model). Strengths include long-form writing, careful reasoning, document analysis (PDFs, images), and code help.

## Best use cases

- Drafting and editing long-form writing (essays, articles, reports)
- Analyzing PDFs, screenshots, and documents
- Explaining technical concepts clearly
- Coding help (especially with reasoning through problems)

## Beginner verdict

Excellent alternative or complement to ChatGPT. Many users prefer Claude's writing style. Try both free tiers and see which you click with.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Free tier daily message limit; refresh window is rolling 5 hours
