## What it does

Claude Pro unlocks ~5× more usage than the free tier, access to Claude Opus (Anthropic's most capable model), the Projects feature (persistent knowledge across conversations), and priority access during high-traffic times.

## Best use cases

- Writers who use Claude as a daily drafting/editing partner
- Long-document analysis (Claude has best-in-class long context)
- Coding help in extended conversations
- Building "Projects" — custom knowledge bases for repeated tasks

## Beginner verdict

Choose Claude Pro over ChatGPT Plus if your main use is writing, document analysis, or thoughtful conversation. Choose ChatGPT Plus if you want image gen, voice, custom GPTs, and a more feature-packed app.

- **Learning curve**: Same as free Claude
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from free Claude account
- **Gotchas**: Even Pro has usage limits (Max tier exists for heavier use)
