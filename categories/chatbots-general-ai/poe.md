## What it does

Poe (by Quora) is a chat platform that aggregates many AI models behind one interface. With one subscription you can chat with GPT-4o, Claude Opus, Gemini Pro, DeepSeek, Llama, Grok, and many community bots. Free tier gives daily message quotas; Pro removes most limits.

## Best use cases

- Comparing how different models answer the same question
- Using premium models without paying for multiple subscriptions
- Building/using custom bots (Poe lets users create chatbots with prompts)
- Trying open-source models without setting up infrastructure

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Limited daily messages across all models |
| Pro | $20/mo | ~1 million compute points/mo — heavy usage across all models |
| Team | $30/user/mo | Workspace features |

*Verify current pricing on the official website — point system has changed before.*

## Ratings from around the web

- **Reddit sentiment**: Liked by enthusiasts; criticized for confusing "compute points" pricing
- **Common feedback**: Best for trying many models; less polished UX than single-model alternatives

*Ratings as of 2026-05. Re-verify periodically.*

## Beginner verdict

Not the first AI subscription to grab. Better fit once you know what you want and need flexibility across models. Free tier is a good way to try multiple models.

- **Learning curve**: Moderate (point system, choosing the right bot)
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Compute points get consumed at different rates per model — premium models cost more per message
