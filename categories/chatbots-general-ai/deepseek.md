## What it does

DeepSeek is a Chinese AI lab that releases its models with open weights and provides free access via web/mobile chat. Their reasoning model (DeepSeek-R1) became famous for matching frontier-model quality at a fraction of the cost. Free tier on the web app has been very generous compared to OpenAI/Anthropic.

## Best use cases

- Replacing ChatGPT Plus for users who hit limits on free tiers
- Developers wanting cheap, capable API access
- Researchers wanting to study/fine-tune open-weights models

## Ratings from around the web

## Beginner verdict

Great free option if you've outgrown ChatGPT's free limits and don't want to pay yet. Note: data sent to DeepSeek's servers is subject to Chinese jurisdiction — avoid for confidential work.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Avoid sending sensitive personal/work data; UI is less polished than Western competitors

## Alternatives

- [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md) — more polished UX
- [Claude](claude.md) — better writing quality
- [Poe](poe.md) — gives access to many models including some open-weights options

## Notes

DeepSeek made headlines in early 2025 when DeepSeek-R1 matched OpenAI's reasoning models at much lower cost, briefly affecting AI-stock prices. Their open-source approach is a notable contrast to OpenAI's closed models.
