## What it does

Perplexity Pro removes the daily Pro search limit. You get unlimited deep web searches powered by your choice of premium model (Claude, GPT-4o, Gemini, etc.), file/PDF uploads with analysis, focus modes (academic, YouTube, Reddit, etc.), and Spaces (collaborative research collections).

## Best use cases

- Writing research papers and citing sources properly
- Deep-dive research on unfamiliar topics
- Analyzing uploaded PDFs/documents with web context
- Replacing both Google + ChatGPT for daily knowledge work
