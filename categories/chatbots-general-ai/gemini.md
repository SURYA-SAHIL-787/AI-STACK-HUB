## What it does

Gemini is Google's AI assistant. Free tier provides access to Gemini Flash and limited Gemini Pro. Integrates natively with Gmail, Google Docs, Sheets, Drive, YouTube, and Maps. Includes image generation, voice mode, and basic research features.

## Best use cases

- Summarizing emails, Google Docs, YouTube videos
- Drafting content directly inside Google Docs and Gmail
- Quick fact lookups (uses Google Search under the hood)
- Students with Google accounts who want one ecosystem

## Pricing breakdown

## Beginner verdict

If you already live in Google's ecosystem (Gmail, Docs, etc.), Gemini's integration is unmatched. Quality is competitive — definitely worth trying alongside ChatGPT and Claude.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Google account (you already have one)
- **Gotchas**: Free tier Pro access is limited; deep features behind paywall
