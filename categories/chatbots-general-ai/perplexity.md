## What it does

Perplexity is a chatbot designed around web search. Every answer comes with cited sources you can click. Free tier offers unlimited basic searches and a few "Pro" searches per day (which use stronger models and dig deeper).

## Best use cases

- Research papers and assignments (you can cite sources)
- Current-events and recent-news questions
- Fact-checking claims from other AI tools
- Quick deep-dives into unfamiliar topics

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |

## Beginner verdict

The single best AI tool for students doing research. Free tier is enough for most assignments. The citation feature alone makes it stand out from ChatGPT/Claude.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Free tier Pro searches reset daily — pace yourself

## Alternatives

- [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md) — has web search but less citation-focused
- [Gemini](gemini.md) — Google-powered search underneath
- [Claude](claude.md) — better for writing the report, not for finding sources
