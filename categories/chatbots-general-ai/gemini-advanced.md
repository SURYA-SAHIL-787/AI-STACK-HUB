## What it does

Google AI Pro (formerly Gemini Advanced) gives you Google's top-tier Gemini models, deep integration in Google Docs/Gmail/Sheets/Drive, NotebookLM Plus, and 2TB of Drive storage — all in one $20/mo bundle. Often the best value among $20/mo AI subscriptions because of the bundled storage.

## Best use cases

- Heavy Google Docs / Gmail / Sheets users who want AI in those apps
- Students storing notes/projects in Drive (the 2TB is genuinely useful)
- Anyone using NotebookLM for studying
- Replacing Google One + ChatGPT Plus with a single subscription

## Beginner verdict

If you'd otherwise pay separately for cloud storage AND an AI chatbot, this bundle is a no-brainer. Quality is competitive with Plus/Pro alternatives.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Upgrade existing Google account
- **Gotchas**: Subscription tier names have rebranded multiple times — confusing
