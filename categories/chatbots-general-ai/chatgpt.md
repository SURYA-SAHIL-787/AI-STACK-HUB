## What it does

ChatGPT is OpenAI's general-purpose conversational AI. The free tier gives you GPT-4o-mini (fast, capable) and limited access to GPT-4o (more capable). Features include text chat, voice mode, image input, basic image generation, web search, and custom GPTs.

## Best use cases

- Answering questions and explaining concepts
- Drafting emails, essays, short content
- Brainstorming and summarizing
- Light coding help, math, translations

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | GPT-4o-mini unlimited + limited GPT-4o, basic features |
| Plus | $20/mo | Higher limits, full GPT-4o + GPT-5 family, advanced voice, image gen | 
| Team | $25–30/user/mo | Workspace, admin, no training on your data |
| Enterprise | Contact sales | Unlimited use, SOC 2, advanced security |

## Beginner verdict

The default first AI chatbot for most people. Free tier is enough to learn how to "talk to AI" — once you outgrow it, upgrade to Plus.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account (email or SSO)
- **Gotchas**: Outputs can be confidently wrong — always verify facts
