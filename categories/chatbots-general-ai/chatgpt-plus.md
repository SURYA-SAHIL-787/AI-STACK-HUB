## What it does

ChatGPT Plus unlocks the full ChatGPT experience: higher message limits on premium models (GPT-4o, GPT-5 family), faster responses, advanced voice mode, generous image generation, file uploads, custom GPTs, and early access to new features.

## Best use cases

- Daily AI use (work, school, side projects)
- Long conversations that hit free tier limits
- Creating custom GPTs for specific recurring workflows
- Using advanced voice mode for hands-free interaction

## Beginner verdict

If you find yourself bumping into free tier limits more than once a week, $20/mo is the easiest AI upgrade decision. Start with free, upgrade when you need it.

- **Learning curve**: Same as free ChatGPT
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from free ChatGPT account
- **Gotchas**: ChatGPT Pro ($200/mo) is a separate, higher tier — not the same thing
