# Buffer (Free)

> Long-running free social scheduler with built-in AI Assistant for drafting posts.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Website** | https://buffer.com |
| **Category** | [Social Media](README.md) |

---

## What it does

Buffer's free tier supports 3 social channels, basic scheduling, and the AI Assistant (drafts posts, repurposes content, generates ideas). Clean, no-fluff UI. Most-loved social scheduler for small teams.

## Best use cases

- Personal brand + 1-2 client side projects
- Repurposing blog content into social
- AI-drafted posts you tweak before publishing
- Replacing manual cross-posting

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 3 channels, basic scheduling, limited AI |
| Essentials | $6/channel/mo | Unlimited AI, analytics |
| Team | $12/channel/mo | Team collaboration |

## Beginner verdict

Best free starting point. Pro at $6/channel is the cheapest serious option.
