## What it does

ChatGPT (or any free chatbot) is great at drafting social posts — give it a topic, audience, tone, and platform, get tailored posts. Many creators use ChatGPT for the writing and Buffer Free for scheduling, replacing $30+/mo "AI social" tools.

## Best use cases

- Drafting platform-specific posts (Twitter thread vs LinkedIn post)
- Repurposing one piece of content into 10 social posts
- Generating hashtags + hooks
- Quick "what should I post today?" prompts

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Use any free chatbot |
| Paid AI | $20/mo | Higher-quality posts with Plus/Pro |

## Beginner verdict

The free combination "ChatGPT + Buffer Free" beats $30/mo "AI social" tools for most solo creators.
