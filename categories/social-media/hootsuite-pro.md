---

## What it does

Professional gives you 10 social channels, full OwlyWriter AI (draft + repurpose + best-times), bulk scheduling, full analytics, and limited listening. Higher tiers add team collaboration and full listening suites.

## Best use cases

- Marketing teams replacing 5 separate tools
- Agencies managing 10+ channels per client
- Brands needing social listening + scheduling combined
- Teams that pre-existed Buffer

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Professional | $99/mo | 1 user, 10 channels |
| Team | $249/mo | 3 users, 20 channels |
| Business | Custom | Enterprise features |

## Beginner verdict

Pricier than Buffer at small scale. Justify with broader feature set or existing team adoption.
