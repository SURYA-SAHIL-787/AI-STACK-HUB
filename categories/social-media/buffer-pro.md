## What it does

Per-channel pricing is unusually fair — most competitors charge a flat monthly fee. $6/channel means a solo creator with 3 channels pays $18/mo for unlimited AI and full features. Includes analytics, hashtag manager, engagement inbox.

## Best use cases

- Solo creators growing beyond free's 3-channel limit
- Indie hackers tracking what posts work
- Replacing $30+/mo competitors with cheaper per-channel pricing
- AI-drafted posts at scale

Best-value paid social tool. Per-channel pricing keeps it cheap for solos.
