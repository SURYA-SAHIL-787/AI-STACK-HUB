## What it does

Taplio is the LinkedIn-specific equivalent of Postwise — AI post ideas, scheduling at best LinkedIn times, "Swipe File" of top-performing posts to inspire your own, CRM-style engagement tracking. Built specifically for LinkedIn algorithm patterns.

## Best use cases

- Founders building B2B audiences on LinkedIn
- Job seekers building presence before placement season
- Sales professionals growing reach
- Consultants positioning themselves as experts
