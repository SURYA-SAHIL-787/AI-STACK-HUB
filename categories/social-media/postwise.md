## What it does

Postwise is laser-focused on X/Twitter — AI generates viral-style tweets and threads, schedules posts at best times, suggests reply opportunities. Trained on patterns from high-performing accounts.

## Best use cases

- Solo creators building a personal brand on X
- Indie hackers building in public
- Newsletter writers cross-posting on X
- Replacing manual "what should I tweet?" anxiety

## Beginner verdict

If X/Twitter is your main platform, Postwise is more focused than Buffer for growth. For multi-platform, stick with Buffer/Vista Social.
