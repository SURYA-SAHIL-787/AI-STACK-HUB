## What it does

Predis generates complete social posts — caption, hashtags, AND graphics — from a single prompt. Strong for visual platforms where you need image + text together. Includes competitor analysis and brand consistency features.

## Best use cases

- Instagram/Facebook visual posts
- Small businesses without designers
- Bulk posting (10 posts at once)
- Competitor inspiration

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |

| Lite | $32/mo | 60 posts/mo, 1 brand |

## Beginner verdict

Useful if you need visual posts and don't want to design in Canva separately. For text-only, ChatGPT + Buffer is cheaper.

## Alternatives

- Canva (with AI, not in this list) — design-focused alternative
- [Buffer](buffer.md) — text-focused
- [SocialBee](socialbee.md) — content variety
