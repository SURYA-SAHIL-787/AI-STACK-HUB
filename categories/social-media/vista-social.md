## What it does

Vista Social is a newer scheduler with broader platform support than Buffer/Hootsuite (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest, Twitch, Mastodon). AI Assistant for post drafting. Generous free tier.

## Best use cases

- Creators on 4+ platforms simultaneously
- TikTok + YouTube Shorts cross-posting
- Indie agencies juggling small clients
- Pinterest-heavy strategies

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 3 social profiles, basic features |
| Pro | $39/mo | 8 profiles, AI Assistant, advanced features |
| Pro Plus | $79/mo | 13 profiles, more features |
| Standard | $149/mo+ | Multi-user team |

## Beginner verdict

Best free option if you post on TikTok, Shorts, or Pinterest (Buffer/Hootsuite are weaker there).
