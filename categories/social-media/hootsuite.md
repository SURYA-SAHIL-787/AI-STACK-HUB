## What it does

Hootsuite is the legacy enterprise-y social platform. OwlyWriter AI drafts posts, finds best post times, repurposes content. Stronger on listening + analytics than Buffer; weaker on per-channel pricing.

## Best use cases

- Agencies managing many clients
- Marketing teams with budget
- Social listening across platforms
- Established workflows that pre-date Buffer

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free Trial | $0 (30-day) | All features for trial period |
| Professional | $99/mo | 1 user, 10 channels |
| Team | $249/mo | 3 users, 20 channels |
| Business | Custom | Enterprise features |

## Beginner verdict

Pricier than Buffer at solo scale. Best for agencies and mid-market teams that need its breadth.
