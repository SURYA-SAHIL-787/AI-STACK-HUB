## What it does

PathAI builds AI models that help pathologists read tissue slides — quantifying biomarkers, supporting cancer diagnosis, and powering clinical trials. Used both in labs serving patient care and by biopharma for drug development.

## Best use cases

- Cancer diagnosis support
- Biomarker quantification (e.g., PD-L1) for oncology
- Clinical trial enrichment and stratification
- Lab efficiency in high-volume pathology workflows

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |

Two sides of the business: lab software for clinical use, and partnerships with pharma for trial work.

## Beginner verdict

Highly specialized — pathology is itself a deep field, and this is infrastructure for that world. Useful to know about as an example of AI shifting how diagnoses are made, even if you never use it directly.
