## What it does

Suki started as a smart medical dictation tool and grew into a full AI assistant — ambient documentation, EHR commands, code suggestion, and patient summaries. Works across Epic, Cerner, Athenahealth, and others, making it friendlier for non-Epic shops.

## Best use cases

- Multi-EHR groups that need a single AI layer
- Clinicians who prefer voice dictation to typing
- Smaller practices that want enterprise-quality scribing
- Specialties with high-volume notes (primary care, urgent care)

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Practice | Per-clinician/month | Voice + ambient note features |
| Enterprise | Custom | Multi-site rollout, integrations, analytics |

## Beginner verdict

A reasonable middle ground between consumer dictation and big-system scribes. Many clinicians ramp up in a few visits — the voice-first design feels familiar to anyone who used Dragon.
