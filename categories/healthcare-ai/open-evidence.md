## What it does

Open Evidence answers clinical questions by pulling from peer-reviewed journals (NEJM, JAMA, and more) and citing every source. Built for point-of-care use — ask a question, get an evidence-backed answer in seconds with linked references.

## Best use cases

- Quick answers at the bedside or in clinic
- Catching up on guidelines you don't use daily
- Resident learning during rounds
- Cross-checking treatment plans against current evidence

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Verified clinician | Free | Full access, requires NPI/credential verification |

Free because it's funded by partnerships with publishers; verification is required to keep it clinician-only.

## Beginner verdict

Great fit for any clinician — the verification step takes a few days, but afterwards it's the fastest way to answer "what does the latest evidence say?" without paywalls.
