## What it does

DAX Copilot is Nuance's ambient documentation product, built on top of decades of medical speech tech and Microsoft's AI stack. Captures the visit conversation, drafts the note, and pushes it back into Epic with one tap.

## Best use cases

- Large Epic-based health systems
- Standardizing scribing across many specialties
- Reducing transcription costs and human scribe headcount
- Combining with Dragon dictation workflows

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | Custom | Per-clinician licensing, Epic integration, change management |

Owned by Microsoft after the Nuance acquisition — pricing typically tracks with Microsoft enterprise health agreements.

## Beginner verdict

The "safe corporate choice" for big systems. If your hospital is on Microsoft + Epic, DAX is usually the path of least resistance.

## Alternatives

- [Abridge](abridge.md) — newer, fast-growing competitor
- [Suki AI](suki.md) — more flexible, multi-EHR
