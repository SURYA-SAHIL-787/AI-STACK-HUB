## What it does

Glass turns a one-line patient case into a differential diagnosis and management plan, with reasoning shown. It is positioned as a thinking-partner for clinicians, not a tool for patients. The free tier covers individual use; teams and health systems get an enterprise tier.

## Best use cases

- Building differentials for tricky presentations
- Teaching tool for residents and students
- Sanity-checking your own clinical reasoning
- Drafting structured assessments and plans

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Individual clinician use |
| Pro | Paid | More requests, advanced features |
| Enterprise | Custom | Health-system deployment, EHR ties |

## Beginner verdict

Best for clinicians who already know their differential-building muscles. Glass amplifies your reasoning rather than replacing it — students should use it to learn, not to skip the thinking.

## Alternatives

- [Open Evidence](open-evidence.md) — evidence search rather than reasoning
- [Abridge](abridge.md) — scribe-focused, not differential-focused
