## What it does

Aidoc runs in the background on imaging studies and flags critical findings — intracranial hemorrhage, pulmonary embolism, c-spine fractures, and many more — pushing prioritized worklists so radiologists read the urgent cases first.

## Best use cases

- Stroke and PE workflows where minutes matter
- High-volume ED radiology
- Trauma centers prioritizing whole-body CTs
- Care coordination across radiology, neurology, and ED
