## What it does

Hippocratic AI builds voice agents that talk to patients for non-diagnostic tasks — appointment prep, medication adherence check-ins, chronic care coaching. Trained and safety-tested with clinicians, deliberately walled off from acute or diagnostic situations.

## Best use cases

- Pre- and post-visit patient calls
- Chronic disease check-ins (diabetes, CHF, COPD)
- Insurance plan onboarding
- Trial recruitment and informed consent prep

## Beginner verdict

Not a tool you use as an individual — it is infrastructure that hospitals and payers deploy on their patient population. Worth knowing about as a reference for what voice AI in healthcare looks like.
