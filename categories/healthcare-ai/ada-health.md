## What it does

Ada walks you through your symptoms with a structured interview, then suggests possible conditions and whether you should seek care. It is not a diagnosis — it is a triage aid built by physicians, available as a free app on iOS and Android.

## Best use cases

- Deciding "do I need to see a doctor?"
- Tracking symptoms over time
- Health literacy for non-medical users
- Pre-visit prep to organize what you want to ask

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer app | Free | Full symptom checker |
| Enterprise | Custom | White-label triage for insurers, hospitals |

## Beginner verdict

Designed for non-medical users — the interview is plain-language and the output always points you toward a real clinician for anything serious. Good to keep on your phone.
