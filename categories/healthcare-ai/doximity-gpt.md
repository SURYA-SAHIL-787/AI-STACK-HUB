## What it does

Doximity GPT lives inside the Doximity app — the largest US physician network — and gives verified clinicians a HIPAA-aware ChatGPT-style assistant. Useful for drafting prior-auth letters, patient instructions, referral notes, and quick reference lookups.

## Best use cases

- Drafting prior authorization appeals
- Writing patient-friendly discharge instructions
- Translating clinical notes into plain English
- Quick reference for guidelines and dosing

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Verified clinician | Free | Full access via Doximity app |

Free for verified members; Doximity monetizes the broader network through recruiting and pharma channels.

## Beginner verdict

If you are already on Doximity, turning this on is a no-brainer. It is intentionally simple — chat box plus templates — and the HIPAA framing makes it safer for daily clinic work than a generic AI tool.
