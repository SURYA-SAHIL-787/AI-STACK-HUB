## What it does

Abridge records doctor–patient conversations and produces a structured note (SOAP, HPI, plan) that the clinician edits and signs. Deeply integrated with Epic and other major EHRs, with strong adoption across US academic medical centers.

## Best use cases

- Reducing clinician note-taking burden
- Faster note completion (often during the visit)
- Multi-specialty deployments across a health system
- Reducing after-hours charting ("pajama time")

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | Custom | Per-clinician licensing, EHR integration, training |

Pricing scales with clinician headcount; significant ROI claims center on reduced burnout and increased throughput.

## Beginner verdict

Not for solo practitioners to buy directly — health systems deploy this. As a clinician, your reaction on first use is usually relief at how much typing disappears.
