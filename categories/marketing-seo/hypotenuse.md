## What it does

Hypotenuse generates marketing content with a focus on e-commerce — product descriptions in bulk, blog posts, ad copy, social posts. Strong on understanding product attributes and writing SEO-friendly e-commerce content. CSV-based bulk generation is the differentiator.

## Best use cases

- Shopify stores generating product descriptions
- E-commerce sellers updating large catalogs
- Marketing agencies producing campaign copy
- Bulk content generation workflows
