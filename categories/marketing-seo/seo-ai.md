## What it does

SEO.ai is a content optimization tool that uses AI to analyze top-ranking pages for any keyword and recommend improvements to your content. Includes brief generation, on-page SEO scoring, and AI writing assistance. Cheaper and simpler than Surfer/Clearscope.

## Best use cases

- Bloggers optimizing posts for SEO without learning complex tools
- Solo content writers needing AI-assisted briefs
- Quick SEO health checks on existing content
- Affordable Surfer alternative
