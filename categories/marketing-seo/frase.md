## What it does

Frase combines content brief generation (analyzes top 20 SERP results for a keyword), AI writing (produces drafts optimized to the brief), and on-page SEO scoring. Cheaper than Surfer with overlapping features. Strong on the "research → brief → write" workflow.

## Best use cases

- Producing data-driven content briefs
- Generating first drafts that need light editing
- Solo writers serving SEO clients
- Bloggers who want briefs without expensive subscriptions
