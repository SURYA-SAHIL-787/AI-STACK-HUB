## What it does

AnswerThePublic shows you the actual questions people search for around any keyword — organized into "who, what, when, where, why, how" wheels plus prepositions, comparisons, and alphabetical variations. AI summary feature surfaces the most valuable angles. Acquired by Neil Patel.

## Best use cases

- Finding blog post topics
- Discovering long-tail keywords
- Understanding what audiences actually want to know
- Generating FAQ content

## Beginner verdict

Best free starting point for content research. 3 searches/day is enough to evaluate; upgrade only if you need bulk research.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
