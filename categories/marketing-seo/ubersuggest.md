## What it does

Ubersuggest is Neil Patel's SEO tool — keyword research, content ideas, site audits, backlink analysis, competitor tracking. Free tier gives 3 searches/day across all features. Lifetime pricing tier is unusually affordable.

## Best use cases

- Learning SEO basics (Neil Patel's content is beginner-friendly)
- Small site audits and quick keyword research
- Replacing expensive enterprise tools for solo projects
- Affordable lifetime SEO toolkit

## Pricing breakdown

## Beginner verdict

Best free SEO tool for learning the basics. If you'll do SEO long-term, the Lifetime tier saves money vs subscriptions.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Free 3-search/day limit; data quality below Ahrefs/SEMrush
