## What it does

Surfer SEO analyzes top-ranking pages for any keyword and gives you a content editor with real-time scoring — keywords to include, ideal word count, headings, related terms. Most-used pro SEO content tool. Includes Surfer AI for end-to-end article generation.

## Best use cases

- Content writers serious about ranking on Google
- SEO agencies producing client content at scale
- Bloggers monetizing via search traffic
- Replacing manual SEO research with data-driven optimization
