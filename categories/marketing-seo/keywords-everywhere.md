## What it does

Keywords Everywhere overlays SEO data on Google searches and other sites — search volume, cost-per-click, competition. Free version shows basic trend data and related keywords; paid credits unlock detailed metrics. Most-installed SEO browser extension.

## Best use cases

- Quickly checking if a topic has search demand
- Finding related keywords while researching
- Competitive content research
- Always-on SEO awareness while browsing
