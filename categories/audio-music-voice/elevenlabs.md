## What it does

ElevenLabs is widely considered the best AI voice generator. It produces speech that's nearly indistinguishable from human recording, supports voice cloning (recreate a specific person's voice from a sample), text-to-speech in 30+ languages, and a "Studio" for long-form audiobook/podcast production. Free tier gives 10,000 characters/month (~10 min of audio).

## Best use cases

- YouTube/TikTok voiceovers without recording yourself
- Audiobook narration
- Podcast generation from written content
- Multilingual content (translate + voice in one workflow)
- Localizing existing videos to new languages

## Beginner verdict

The single best free TTS tool. 10,000 chars/month is enough to make several short videos. Upgrade to Starter ($5) if you want voice cloning — one of the cheapest premium AI tools.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Voice cloning is paid-tier only; commercial use requires Creator+ tier
