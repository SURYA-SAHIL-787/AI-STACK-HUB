## What it does

Udio is Suno's main competitor — also generates full songs from text prompts, but focuses on higher audio fidelity and remix workflows. Lets you extend tracks, swap sections, and re-generate parts. Founded by ex-Google DeepMind researchers.

## Best use cases

- Higher audio quality than Suno for production work
- Remixing AI-generated tracks (extend, swap parts)
- Comparing AI music outputs across tools
- Musicians using AI for inspiration/demos

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | ~10 songs/day, non-commercial |
| Standard | $10/mo | 1,200 credits/mo (~120 songs) |
| Pro | $30/mo | 4,800 credits, higher quality, commercial use |

*See [Udio Standard page](udio-standard.md) for paid tier details.*

## Beginner verdict

Try both Suno and Udio free tiers — quality preference varies by genre and personal taste. Many users use both for different songs.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Same lawsuit risks as Suno; commercial use requires paid tier
