## What it does

The $5 ElevenLabs Starter tier is unusually cheap for an AI subscription. You get 30,000 chars/month (~30 min of audio), Instant Voice Cloning (recreate a voice from a 1-minute sample), all standard voices, and most features unlocked. Commercial use is allowed at this tier.

## Best use cases

- Cloning your own voice for content creation
- Cloning a character voice for fiction/audiobooks
- Multilingual voiceovers in your own voice
- Best entry point for serious ElevenLabs use
