## What it does

Descript's killer feature: edit audio/video by editing the auto-generated transcript. Delete a word in the transcript, the audio deletes that word. Type new words, AI voice cloning fills them in (Overdub). Includes screen recording, AI eye contact correction, filler-word removal (um, uh, like), and multi-track podcast editing.

## Best use cases

- Podcast editing (fastest workflow in the industry)
- YouTube video editing for talking-head content
- Removing filler words automatically
- Creating tutorials with screen recording + voiceover

## Beginner verdict

If you record yourself talking (podcasts, YouTube), Descript will save you hours per episode. Free tier is enough to evaluate the workflow.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account + desktop app
- **Gotchas**: Overdub voice cloning requires Pro tier
