## What it does

Udio Standard gives you 1,200 credits per month (~120 songs), faster generation queue, and higher concurrent generations. Commercial-use clarity varies — check current terms on the website.

## Best use cases

- Music producers using AI for demos and ideas
- Daily Udio users hitting free limits
- Generating large numbers of song variations
- Higher-fidelity AI music for production

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | $10/mo | 1,200 credits/mo |
| Pro | $30/mo | 4,800 credits, higher quality, commercial use |

## Beginner verdict

For most casual users, Suno Pro at $10 is better-known. Udio Standard is worth picking if you prefer its sound on free tier comparisons.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from free Udio
- **Gotchas**: Confirm commercial-rights terms before using outputs commercially
