## What it does

Suno generates complete songs from text descriptions — lyrics, melody, vocals, instrumentation. You can give it a topic ("a sad ballad about losing your keys"), a genre, custom lyrics, or a vocal style. Free tier offers 10 songs per day. Many viral AI music tracks of 2024–2025 were made in Suno.

## Best use cases

- Background music for videos (without licensing concerns)
- Custom songs for events, birthdays, weddings
- Songwriting drafts and idea exploration
- Memes and viral content

## Beginner verdict

If you've never made AI music, start here. The 10 songs/day free tier is plenty to experiment. Upgrade to Pro only if you want commercial rights.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Free tier outputs are non-commercial; commercial use requires Pro
