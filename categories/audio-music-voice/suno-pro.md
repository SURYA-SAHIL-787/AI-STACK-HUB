## What it does

Suno Pro removes the 10-song-per-day free limit (gives you 500/month), grants commercial-use rights to your generated songs, provides faster generation, and unlocks the latest model versions first. The $10 price is competitive with single-AI-tool subscriptions.

## Best use cases

- YouTube/TikTok creators using AI music in commercial content
- Podcasters needing custom intro/outro music
- Music producers using AI for demos and ideation
- Daily song generation hobbyists

## Beginner verdict

If you use AI music in monetized content (YouTube, podcasts, streaming), Pro is worth it just for the commercial rights — free tier doesn't allow commercial use.

- **Learning curve**: Same as free Suno
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from free account
- **Gotchas**: Music label lawsuits ongoing; commercial rights from Suno don't necessarily shield you legally
