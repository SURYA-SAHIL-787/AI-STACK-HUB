## What it does

Microsoft offers free DALL-E 3 image generation via Bing Image Creator and Microsoft Designer. You get a daily allotment of "boosts" (faster generation) plus unlimited slower generation. Quality matches DALL-E 3 inside ChatGPT Plus.

## Best use cases

- Trying DALL-E 3 quality without paying for ChatGPT Plus
- Quick visual concepts and social media content
- Replacement for paid stock images in casual contexts
- Children and students (free, safe defaults)

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | ~15 fast "boost" generations/day + unlimited slower ones |
| Microsoft 365 Copilot Pro | $20/mo | Higher boost limit, plus Copilot features in Office |

*Verify current limits on the official website — boost count has changed before.*

## Beginner verdict

If you only need image generation occasionally, this is the no-brainer pick — free, polished, and DALL-E 3 quality. Use Bing Image Creator if you want simple, or Microsoft Designer for more layout/template features.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free Microsoft account
- **Gotchas**: Content filter is stricter than other tools — some prompts blocked
