## What it does

Craiyon (formerly DALL-E Mini) is one of the original free AI image generators that went viral in 2022. It still operates as a no-signup free tool with significantly lower quality than modern alternatives — but for first-time experimentation, it's the lowest-friction option.

## Best use cases

- First-time AI image generation (zero commitment)
- Meme creation (the "Craiyon look" is recognizable)
- Showing kids/non-tech-savvy people what AI image gen does
- Quick weird-quality outputs for fun

## Pricing breakdown

## Beginner verdict

If you've never tried AI image generation before, Craiyon is the absolute lowest barrier — no signup, just type and click. For anything real, use Bing Image Creator or Leonardo instead.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: None (no account)
- **Gotchas**: Quality is dated; expect blurry, distorted faces
