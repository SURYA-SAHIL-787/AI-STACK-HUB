## What it does

Leonardo provides a polished UI on top of Stable Diffusion / Flux models with custom-trained variants for game assets, character art, illustration, and photorealism. Free tier offers ~150 fast tokens/day (enough for ~30 images), plus features like image-to-image, inpainting, upscale, and a "Realtime Canvas" sketch-to-image mode.

## Best use cases

- Game asset generation (characters, items, environments)
- Concept art and mood boards
- Trying many image styles without paying
- Quick mockups for product/marketing visuals
