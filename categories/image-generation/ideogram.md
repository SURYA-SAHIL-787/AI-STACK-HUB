## What it does

Ideogram specializes in text-in-image — the area where most other image generators (Midjourney, DALL-E) traditionally struggled. Lets you generate posters, logos, signs, T-shirt designs, and meme-style content with readable, intentional typography.

## Best use cases

- Poster and flyer design
- Logo concepts and brand mockups
- T-shirt designs and merchandise
- Memes with text that actually reads correctly

## Beginner verdict

Worth knowing about even if you mainly use other tools. If you ever need to generate an image with text (poster, ad, meme), Ideogram is the right choice.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Free tier images may be public by default
