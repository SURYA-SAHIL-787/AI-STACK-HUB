## What it does

Midjourney is the AI image generator most associated with "wow factor" output. Strong on photorealism, fantasy art, concept design, fashion, and editorial-style imagery. Originally Discord-based, now has a web interface. Continuously updates its model (currently v6+).

## Best use cases

- Concept art for games, films, books
- Editorial and marketing imagery
- High-quality social media visuals
- Reference imagery for design work

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |

## Ratings from around the web

- **Reddit sentiment**: Almost universally praised for image quality; criticized for Discord UX (less so now with web UI)
- **Common feedback**: Best output among current tools, but more expensive than alternatives

*Ratings as of 2026-05. Re-verify periodically.*

## Beginner verdict

Worth paying for if AI imagery is a regular part of your work or hobby. Free tier no longer exists (discontinued years ago). Best evaluated through a Basic month subscription.

- **Learning curve**: Easy (web UI) / Moderate (Discord)
- **Setup required**: Paid account; web UI requires connecting Discord
- **Gotchas**: No free tier; commercial use requires Standard tier or above on some plans
