## What it does

Adobe Firefly is Adobe's family of AI image, video, and design models. Key differentiator: trained only on Adobe Stock content, openly licensed material, and public-domain — making generated images cleared for commercial use without copyright concerns. Integrated deeply into Photoshop (Generative Fill), Illustrator, and Express.

## Best use cases

- Commercial design work where copyright clarity matters
- Photoshop users (Generative Fill is the killer feature)
- Brand marketing assets
- Replacing stock photos for client work

## Beginner verdict

If you already use Adobe products, Firefly is free to try and integrates seamlessly. For pure image gen without Adobe ecosystem, alternatives produce better output for less money.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Adobe ID (free)
- **Gotchas**: Generative credits run out faster than you expect on the standalone plans

## Alternatives

- [Midjourney](midjourney.md) — better aesthetic, less commercial-safe
