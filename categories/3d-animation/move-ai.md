## What it does

Move.ai turns ordinary phone video into 3D motion capture data — no markers, no suits, no studio. Upload a clip, get fbx animation data. Used by indie studios and increasingly by Hollywood for previz.

## Best use cases

- Indie game character animations from real movement
- Music video previz
- Animation reels without mocap studio budget
- Capturing your own movement for animation

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Pay-as-you-go | $15+/clip | One-off mocap |
| Subscription | $30+/mo | Bundle of clips/month |
| Enterprise | Custom | Studio-scale + multi-cam |

## Beginner verdict

Best mocap solution for solo/indie creators today. Watch the pricing if you use it heavily.

## Alternatives

- [Cascadeur](cascadeur.md) — pure animation, no mocap
- Rokoko Vision — competing markerless mocap
- DeepMotion Animate 3D — competing solo-friendly mocap
