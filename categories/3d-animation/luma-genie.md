## What it does

Genie is Luma Labs' text-to-3D tool. Type a prompt, get a 3D model in ~10 seconds. Runs in Discord and on the web. Free use is generous — Luma's main paid product is video AI (Dream Machine), not Genie.

## Best use cases

- Quick 3D experiments in Discord
- Visual ideation before building in Blender
- Hobby projects without paid tools
- Pairing with Luma's video AI for full workflows

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Generous free generations |
| Luma paid plans | $30/mo+ | Video generation (Dream Machine) |

## Beginner verdict

Easiest entry to text→3D. Free, fast, friendly UI.
