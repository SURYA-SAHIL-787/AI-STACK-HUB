## What it does

Cascadeur is a 3D character animation tool with AI/physics assistance. Pose a character, and it auto-balances and fills frames between key poses. Indie license is free up to a revenue threshold.

## Best use cases

- Indie game character animation
- Animation reels and short films
- Faster keyframing for action sequences
- Animators learning physics-aware workflows

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Indie (Free) | $0 | Full features up to $100k/yr revenue |
| Pro | $25/mo | Commercial use above revenue cap |
| Business | Custom | Studio-scale licensing |

## Beginner verdict

Surprisingly powerful free indie tier. Strong second tool after Blender for animation.

- [Move.ai](move-ai.md) — mocap-driven alternative
