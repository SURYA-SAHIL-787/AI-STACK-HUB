## What it does

Kaedim turns 2D concept art into production-quality 3D meshes — with clean topology, UV unwrap, and game-engine readiness. Output is much closer to handmade quality than free tools, at a price.

## Best use cases

- Studios producing real games (not just prototypes)
- Concept-art-driven pipelines
- Cutting weeks of artist time per asset
- Outsourcing 3D without hiring contractors

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Indie | $300/mo | Production-quality meshes, limits apply |
| Studio | Custom | Higher volume + enterprise SLAs |

## Beginner verdict

Not for hobbyists. The right call when free tools' quality is blocking your shipping.

## Alternatives

- [Meshy](meshy.md) — much cheaper, lower quality
- [Sloyd](sloyd.md) — procedural for game-ready output
- Hire a 3D artist on Upwork — comparable cost, slower
