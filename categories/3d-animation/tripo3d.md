## What it does

Tripo3D generates 3D meshes from text prompts or single images. Output quality is competitive with Meshy, and the free tier is similarly generous. Good support for downloading in glb/fbx/obj.

## Best use cases

- Prototyping props and characters fast
- AR experiences needing one-off 3D objects
- Side-by-side with Meshy to A/B which gives better results
- Hobby projects with no budget

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 600 starter credits |
| Plus | $10/mo | 1000 credits/mo |
| Pro | $30/mo | 4000 credits/mo |

## Beginner verdict

Worth trying alongside Meshy — different prompts work better on different tools.
