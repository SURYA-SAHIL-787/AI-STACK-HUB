## What it does

Luphra turns a prompt, sketch, or references into an editable 3D design in the browser, then helps quote and manufacture the finished object. The point is prompt-to-matter — not just a mesh download.

## Best use cases

- Turning a product idea into an editable 3D model without CAD
- Prototyping physical objects you can print or manufacture
- Sketch-to-3D for makers and product designers
- Going past a mesh file to a quoted, produced part

## Beginner verdict

No CAD experience required. Start with a prompt or sketch, then edit shape, dimensions, color, and material in the browser.
