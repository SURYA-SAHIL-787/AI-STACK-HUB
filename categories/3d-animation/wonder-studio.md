---

## What it does

Upload a video of a person acting; Wonder Studio replaces them with a 3D character, automatically handling tracking, lighting match, and animation. Output works directly in Blender, Maya, or Unreal.

## Best use cases

- Indie VFX-heavy filmmaking
- Replacing an actor with a fantasy/sci-fi creature
- Animation tests with real performance reference
- Music videos with stylized characters

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Lite | $20/mo | 720p, limited credits |
| Pro | $70/mo | 1080p, more credits |

## Beginner verdict

Niche but magical when it fits your workflow. Try the Lite plan first.
