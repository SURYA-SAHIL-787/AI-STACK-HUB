## What it does

Meshy turns text prompts or single images into 3D meshes you can download as glb/fbx/obj. Free tier gives 200 credits per month. Quality has jumped significantly in 2025 — good enough for game prototyping.

## Best use cases

- Prototyping game assets without a 3D artist
- Generating props for AR/VR projects
- Concept art → 3D for previz
- Hobby game jams

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 200 credits/mo, watermark-free |
| Pro | $20/mo | 1000 credits, faster |
| Max | $60/mo | 4000 credits, priority queue |

## Beginner verdict

Best free starting point for text→3D. Try it before paying.
