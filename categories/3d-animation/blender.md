## What it does

Blender is the industry-grade free alternative to Maya/3ds Max. Modeling, sculpting, rigging, animation, simulation, rendering. AI add-ons (Stable Diffusion, AI Render, BlenderGPT) extend it for texturing and generation.

## Best use cases

- Real production 3D work (free)
- Combining AI tools with manual modeling
- Animation reels, short films, game assets
- Learning 3D without paying for licenses

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Full Blender, forever |

Paid AI add-ons exist (~$5-30 one-time on Blender Market).

## Beginner verdict

If you're serious about 3D, you'll end up here. Steep learning curve.

## Alternatives

- [Spline AI](spline.md) — easier UI, focused on web/UI 3D
- Autodesk Maya — paid industry incumbent
- Cinema 4D — paid, motion graphics focus
