## What it does

Spline is a browser-based 3D design tool — Figma-like UX for 3D. AI features generate objects, animations, and textures from prompts. Used heavily for landing-page 3D and interactive web hero graphics.

## Best use cases

- 3D hero illustrations for landing pages
- Animated 3D for marketing sites
- Designers who want 3D without learning Blender
- Embeddable 3D scenes in web apps

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 3 editor scenes, AI limited |
| Basic | $9/mo | Unlimited scenes, full AI |
| Super | $19/mo | Team features, more exports |

## Beginner verdict

Best paid pick for designers — fastest way to ship 3D on a website.

## Alternatives

- [Blender](blender.md) — full power, steeper learning
- Vectary — competing browser 3D tool
- [Meshy](meshy.md) — model generation only, not a designer
