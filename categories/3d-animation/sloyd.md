## What it does

Sloyd uses procedural + AI generation to make 3D models with sliders (size, complexity, style). Unlike pure-generative tools, the meshes are clean and optimized for game engines.

## Best use cases

- Game prototyping (clean topology)
- Rapidly iterating asset variants
- Unity/Unreal-ready models without manual cleanup
- Indie studios saving on artist time

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Limited downloads, in-browser preview |
| Hobbyist | $14/mo | Unlimited downloads, basic license |
| Studio | $50/mo | Commercial license, more formats |

## Beginner verdict

Most "game-ready" output of any free 3D AI tool. Hobbyist tier is fair value.
