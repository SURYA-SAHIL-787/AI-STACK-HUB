## What it does

Quizlet started as a digital flashcard app and is now an AI-enhanced study platform. Free tier gives you unlimited flashcard creation, access to 500M+ student-made study sets, basic study modes (learn, match, test), and limited AI features (Magic Notes — turn a doc into a study set).

## Best use cases

- Vocabulary memorization (foreign languages, exam prep)
- Definitions and short-answer terms
- Reviewing flashcards on your phone during commutes
- Borrowing study sets from other students for common courses

## Beginner verdict

If your study material involves memorizing terms, Quizlet's free tier is enough. For full AI features (Q-Chat tutor), you'll need Plus.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Many "free" features got moved to Plus over time
