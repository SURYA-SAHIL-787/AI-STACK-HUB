## What it does

NotebookLM is Google's "AI research assistant" — you upload up to ~50 sources per notebook (PDFs, Google Docs, websites, YouTube videos, copy-pasted text), and Gemini reads them and answers questions, generates summaries, creates study guides, drafts an FAQ, and even produces audio "podcast" conversations between two AI hosts about your materials.

## Best use cases

- Turning a semester's worth of lecture slides into a study guide
- Summarizing research papers for literature reviews
- Generating audio podcasts to "listen" to study materials while commuting
- Asking questions grounded in your specific course content (with citations)

## Beginner verdict

If you're studying anything, try NotebookLM. The free tier alone is one of the best study tools available. Upload your textbook chapters, lecture slides, and any reference PDFs — then ask questions or generate a study guide.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Google account
- **Gotchas**: Source upload size limits; very long PDFs may need to be split
