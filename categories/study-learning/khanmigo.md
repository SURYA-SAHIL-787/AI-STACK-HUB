## What it does

Khanmigo is an AI tutor built on top of GPT-4 with Khan Academy's pedagogy baked in. Instead of giving you the answer, it walks you through problems with Socratic questions — helping you figure it out. Covers all Khan Academy subjects (math, science, humanities, computing). Available to learners ($4/mo) and free to teachers + many districts.

## Best use cases

- K-12 students working through Khan Academy lessons
- Learners who want to be guided to answers, not given them
- Math practice with personalized help
- Test prep (SAT, AP exams, etc.)

## Beginner verdict

If you (or someone you know) actively uses Khan Academy for learning, Khanmigo's $4/mo is a fair price. For self-directed study outside the Khan curriculum, general chatbots are more flexible.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Khan Academy account + Khanmigo subscription
- **Gotchas**: Tied to Khan Academy curriculum — not a general-purpose chatbot
