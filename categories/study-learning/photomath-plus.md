## What it does

Photomath uses your phone camera to scan math problems (handwritten or printed) and produces step-by-step solutions. Free tier shows answers + basic steps. Plus tier adds detailed explanations, animated tutorials, and AI-powered "Why?" follow-ups at each step.

## Best use cases

- Checking your work on homework problems
- Understanding HOW to solve problems, not just the answer
- Calculus and advanced math where steps are critical
- Self-study during exam prep

## Beginner verdict

If math is your weak subject, Photomath Plus is genuinely useful — the step explanations help you LEARN, not just get answers. For one-off problems, free tier is fine.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Install app
- **Gotchas**: Don't use as a shortcut around understanding — that'll bite you on exams
