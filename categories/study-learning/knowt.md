## What it does

Knowt was built as a "free Quizlet alternative" by college students. Free features include AI-generated flashcards from notes, practice tests, AP/IB/GCSE study guides, and a chrome extension that imports Quizlet sets. AI features (smart flashcards, explanations) are included free.

## Best use cases

- Quizlet's free tier doesn't cover what you need
- AP, IB, and GCSE exam prep (lots of curated content)
- Auto-generating flashcards from class notes
- Importing your existing Quizlet sets

## Beginner verdict

If you used Quizlet and got annoyed by Plus paywalls, Knowt is the natural switch. Free tier gives you what Quizlet Plus charges for.

- **Learning curve**: Easy (Quizlet-like UX)
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Smaller user base than Quizlet — fewer pre-made sets for niche subjects
