## What it does

Quizlet Plus unlocks the AI features Quizlet pulled behind a paywall: Q-Chat (AI tutor that asks you questions and explains concepts), full Magic Notes (turn any document into a study set), unlimited Quizlet AI-generated study modes, offline access, no ads.

## Best use cases

- Students relying on Quizlet for daily study
- Exam prep where you want the AI tutor to drill you
- Frequent flyers who need offline study mode
- Replacing several other AI study tools with Quizlet's full suite

## Beginner verdict

If you study from Quizlet several times a week, $7.99/mo is reasonable. For casual use, free Quizlet or Knowt is enough.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from free Quizlet account
- **Gotchas**: Cancel before yearly renewal if you don't end up using it daily
