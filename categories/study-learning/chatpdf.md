## What it does

ChatPDF lets you upload any PDF and have a conversation with it. The AI reads the document and answers questions, generates summaries, finds specific quotes, and explains concepts. Designed for the specific use case of "I have this long PDF and need to understand it without reading every page."

## Best use cases

- Studying from textbook chapters
- Reviewing research papers quickly
- Finding specific information in long manuals or reports
- Generating study questions from a chapter

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 2 PDFs/day, up to 120 pages, 50 questions/day |
| Plus | $5/mo | 50 PDFs/day, up to 2,000 pages each, unlimited questions |

## Beginner verdict

If you regularly read long PDFs (textbooks, papers, manuals), the $5/mo Plus tier pays for itself fast. Free tier is enough to evaluate.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Page/question limits on free tier hit fast on academic work
