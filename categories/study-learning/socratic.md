## What it does

Socratic is Google's free homework-help app. Take a photo of any problem (math, science, history, English) and it uses AI to identify the problem, explain how to solve it, and link to relevant videos and articles. Covers most school subjects through high school.

## Best use cases

- Quick homework help when stuck on a problem
- Understanding concepts you missed in class
- Step-by-step math problem explanations
- Finding relevant educational videos automatically

## Beginner verdict

Best for school-age students. For college/professional study, use NotebookLM or ChatPDF instead — Socratic is optimized for K-12 curricula.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Install the app, no account needed
- **Gotchas**: Limited utility beyond high-school subjects
