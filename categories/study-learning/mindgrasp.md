## What it does

Mindgrasp accepts almost any input — YouTube videos, lecture recordings, PDFs, web pages, podcasts — and turns them into structured notes, flashcards, quizzes, and answers to questions you ask. The video processing is the differentiator — most study AI tools can't handle long lecture videos efficiently.

## Best use cases

- Processing 2-hour lecture recordings into 10 minutes of notes
- Mixing PDF readings + video lectures into one study session
- Generating practice quizzes from class materials
- Students with auditory learning materials (podcasts, lectures)

## Beginner verdict

If your courses are video-heavy (online courses, recorded lectures), Mindgrasp's $9.99 is worth it. If you mostly study from PDFs, NotebookLM Free covers you.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Free tier is very limited — you'll hit caps fast with real coursework

## Alternatives

- [NotebookLM](notebooklm.md) — free, strong on PDFs, weaker on long videos
- [StudyFetch](studyfetch.md) — broader suite at higher price
