## What it does

Fathom is unusually generous — unlimited meetings, transcripts, AI summaries, action items, and recording all free for individual use. The paid Team tier adds CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot), Slack/Notion sharing, advanced analytics, and centralized billing.

## Best use cases

- Solo consultants who want all-you-can-eat recording
- Anyone outgrowing Otter free tier but resisting subscription
- Recording client calls for solo professional service businesses
- Sales reps at small companies (Team tier adds CRM)

## Beginner verdict

Best free meeting tool for solo professionals — actually unlimited, no gotchas. Use this before paying for Otter or Fireflies.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account + browser extension
- **Gotchas**: Bot joins meetings — same etiquette concerns; free tier is for individuals only
