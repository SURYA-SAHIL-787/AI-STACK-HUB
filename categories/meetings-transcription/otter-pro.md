## What it does

Otter Pro expands the free tier — 1,200 transcription minutes/month, 90-minute per-conversation limit (up from 30), advanced Otter AI Chat for asking questions across all your meetings, custom vocabulary, and priority transcription.

## Best use cases

- Daily meeting users (transcribing 5+ meetings/week)
- Recording long meetings (workshops, all-hands, training sessions)
- Searching across years of meeting history via AI Chat
- Students recording multiple long lectures per week

## Beginner verdict

If you've hit the free 300 min/mo limit, annual Otter Pro at $8.33/mo is reasonable. For occasional use, free is enough.

- **Learning curve**: Same as free Otter
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from free Otter
- **Gotchas**: Annual price is much lower than monthly — pay annually if committed
