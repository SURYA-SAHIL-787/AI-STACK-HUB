## What it does

Granola takes a different approach — no bot joins your meeting. Instead, you type rough notes during the call; Granola listens via your Mac's audio and uses AI to enhance your notes after the call with full context. Result: meeting notes that feel like your notes but with the detail of a transcript.

## Best use cases

- Professionals who don't want a bot in their meetings
- Consultants and sales reps who need polished notes
- People who prefer to stay engaged (typing during the call) rather than relying purely on transcription
- Mac users (currently primary platform)

## Beginner verdict

The most-loved newer meeting tool. If you're on Mac and hate bots in your calls, try the free 25 notes. Windows users wait for the beta to mature.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Mac app install, grant audio permissions
- **Gotchas**: Mac-only currently; free tier is 25 lifetime, not monthly
