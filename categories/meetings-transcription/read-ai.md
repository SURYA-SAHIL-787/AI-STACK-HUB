## What it does

Read.ai differentiates with analytics beyond pure transcription — it scores meetings on engagement, sentiment, and pacing; tracks how much each participant spoke; identifies questions, action items, and key decisions. Includes email and chat analytics in paid tiers.

## Best use cases

- Managers wanting data on team meeting effectiveness
- Improving meeting culture (Read shows "speaking time imbalance")
- 1-on-1 coaching and feedback loops
- Sales/CSM teams analyzing customer conversations

## Beginner verdict

Best if you specifically want meeting metrics (not just transcription). For pure note-taking, Otter or Granola are simpler.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account + browser extension
- **Gotchas**: Some teams find the analytics intrusive — communicate with attendees
