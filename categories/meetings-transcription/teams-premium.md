## What it does

Teams Premium adds AI features to Microsoft Teams meetings — Intelligent Recap (auto chapters, summary, action items, mentions of you), live translation (40+ languages), advanced webinar features, and meeting templates. Tightly integrated with the Microsoft ecosystem (Outlook, SharePoint, Loop).

## Best use cases

- Enterprises standardized on Microsoft Teams
- Multinational teams needing live translation
- Webinar / town hall AI summaries
- Replacing Otter/Fireflies when company already pays for Microsoft 365

## Beginner verdict

Only worth it if your company already uses Teams as the primary meeting tool. Cross-platform users get more value from Otter/Fireflies.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Add-on purchase through Microsoft 365 admin
- **Gotchas**: Pricing tiers and bundles change frequently — verify what's actually included
