## What it does

Fireflies Pro unlocks unlimited AI summaries, full meeting history, advanced search across all transcripts, AI-powered topic tracking, CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive), and Smart Search (asks questions about all meetings).

## Best use cases

- Sales teams logging calls to CRM automatically
- Customer success managers tracking customer mentions across calls
- Recruiters reviewing dozens of interview calls
- Consultants billing time across multiple client meetings

## Beginner verdict

For sales/CSM roles where calls feed your pipeline, $18 is reasonable. For general meeting note-taking, Otter Pro is cheaper.

- **Learning curve**: Same as free Fireflies
- **Setup required**: Upgrade + connect CRM
- **Gotchas**: True value requires the CRM integration — set that up first
