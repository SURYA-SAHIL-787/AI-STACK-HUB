## What it does

Otter joins your Zoom/Meet/Teams calls (as a participant or recording bot), transcribes in real time, generates summaries with action items, and lets you search every meeting you've ever had. Otter AI Chat lets you ask questions about your meetings ("what did the client say about the timeline?").

## Best use cases

- Recording and transcribing university lectures
- Meeting notes for client calls / team standups
- Searching past meetings for "did we agree to X?"
- Sharing meeting summaries with people who couldn't attend

## Beginner verdict

For students, Otter is the go-to free tool — record your lectures, get searchable transcripts. For meeting-heavy professionals, you'll outgrow the free tier quickly.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Bot joining your meetings may need disclosure depending on jurisdiction
