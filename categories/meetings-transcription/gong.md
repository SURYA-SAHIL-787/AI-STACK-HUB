## What it does

Gong is the most-known revenue intelligence platform. It records sales calls, transcribes them, analyzes them with AI for buyer sentiment, talking patterns, competitor mentions, and deal risk — then coaches reps with what's working. Used at scale by sales teams from Series B startups to Fortune 500.

## Best use cases

- Sales coaching at scale (manager listens to 1, AI reviews all)
- Forecasting deals based on conversational signals
- Competitive intelligence (what are buyers saying about competitors?)
- Onboarding new reps with greatest-hits call library

## Beginner verdict

Not an individual tool — enterprise-only. If you work at a company using Gong, learn it. Otherwise, it's not relevant for personal use.

- **Learning curve**: Moderate (lots of features and workflows)
- **Setup required**: Company-wide deployment, CRM integration, training
- **Gotchas**: Pricing is opaque — negotiate hard; bundle with adjacent tools where possible
