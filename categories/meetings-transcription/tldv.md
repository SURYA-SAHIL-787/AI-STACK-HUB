## What it does

tl;dv ("too long; didn't view") records your meetings with one click, generates transcripts, and produces AI summaries. The free tier is unusually generous — unlimited recordings, transcripts, and AI summaries for 1-on-1 meetings. Team meetings have some restrictions.

## Best use cases

- Recording 1-on-1 meetings (career conversations, interviews, coaching)
- Quick meeting clips and timestamps for sharing
- Free alternative to Otter/Fireflies if you don't need bot-based workflow
- Capturing user interviews for product/UX research

## Beginner verdict

If you record meetings occasionally and don't want a subscription, tl;dv's free tier is hard to beat. For daily meeting-heavy use, Otter Pro or Fireflies Pro have better workflows.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Browser extension + free account
- **Gotchas**: Some advanced features moved to paid tier over time
