## What it does

Fireflies "Fred" bot auto-joins meetings on your calendar, records audio, transcribes, generates AI summaries with action items, and pushes notes to Notion/Slack/CRM. Free tier includes unlimited transcription minutes but limits AI summary credits and storage.

## Best use cases

- Auto-capture all calendar meetings without thinking
- Sales reps logging calls to CRM
- Distributed teams with attendees in different time zones
- "I missed that meeting, what did we decide?"

## Beginner verdict

If your day is full of back-to-back calls, Fireflies removes the "take notes vs participate" tradeoff. For students, Otter is simpler.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account + connect calendar
- **Gotchas**: Auto-joining bot creates etiquette concerns; communicate with meeting hosts
