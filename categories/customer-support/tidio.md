## What it does

Tidio gives you a chat widget + AI bot (Lyro) that answers common questions from your help docs. Free tier covers 50 conversations a month, integrates with Shopify, WordPress, and email. Easy install — copy-paste a script tag.

## Best use cases

- E-commerce stores answering shipping/return questions
- SaaS landing pages capturing leads
- Replacing static "Contact us" forms
- First-touch AI deflection before a human

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 50 conversations/mo, basic Lyro AI |
| Starter | $29/mo | 100 conversations, more channels |
| Lyro AI | $39/mo | 50 Lyro AI conversations |

## Beginner verdict

Easiest AI chatbot to set up. The free tier is generous for small sites.
