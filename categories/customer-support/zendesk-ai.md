## What it does

Zendesk Suite + AI add-on gives you AI agents (Zendesk AI Agents), intent detection on incoming tickets, auto-suggested macros, and an admin AI copilot. The default choice if you're already on Zendesk.

## Best use cases

- Established Zendesk shops adding AI on top
- Enterprise support with strict compliance needs
- Multi-brand support orgs
- Pairing AI deflection with omnichannel

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Suite Team | $55/agent/mo | Base ticketing |
| Suite Pro | $115/agent/mo | AI add-ons available |
| Suite Enterprise | $169/agent/mo | Full AI suite, custom |

## Beginner verdict

If you're already on Zendesk, adding AI is the obvious move. Don't start here from scratch.
