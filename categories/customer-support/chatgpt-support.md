---

## What it does

Not a dedicated support tool, but heavily used by support agents to draft empathetic replies, translate messages, summarize long threads, and build response macros. Free tier is enough for most use.

## Best use cases

- Drafting first-pass replies to tricky tickets
- Translating customer messages to your language
- Building a library of canned responses
- Summarizing long escalations before handoff

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | GPT-5 mini, image input, limited use |
| Plus | $20/mo | GPT-5 full, faster, advanced voice |
| Team | $25/user/mo | Shared workspace, admin |

## Beginner verdict

Every support agent should have this open in a tab. Free tier is enough.
