## What it does

Crisp is a free shared inbox for chat, email, and social DMs. MagicReply (their AI) suggests responses based on prior conversations. Lighter, more affordable than Intercom for small teams.

## Best use cases

- Solo founder managing chat + email in one place
- Multi-channel support without paying enterprise rates
- AI suggestions to speed up reply time
- Replacing a shared Gmail or Slack channel for support

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 2 seats, chat + email + basic AI |
| Mini | $25/mo | Multi-channel, file sharing |
| Essentials | $99/mo | Full AI MagicReply, integrations |

## Beginner verdict

Best free pick for solo founders — 2 seats is enough for early stage.
