## What it does

Upload your PDFs, paste your help docs, or link your website — Chatbase trains a chatbot on that content. Embed the widget on your site. Free tier covers 1 chatbot with ~30 messages/mo.

## Best use cases

- "Ask our docs" widget on a SaaS site
- Internal knowledge-base bot for your team
- FAQ deflection before tickets reach humans
- Demo bots for client projects

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 1 bot, 30 messages/mo |
| Hobby | $40/mo | 2 bots, 2000 messages |
| Standard | $150/mo | 5 bots, 10k messages |

## Beginner verdict

Easiest way to build a docs-trained chatbot. Free tier is enough to demo.
