## What it does

Forethought layers AI on top of Zendesk, Salesforce, or Freshdesk. It deflects tickets with Solve, triages with Triage, and assists agents with Assist. Strong at routing tickets to the right team automatically.

## Best use cases

- Existing Zendesk/Salesforce shops adding AI without switching
- High ticket volume with messy intent classification
- Reducing avg handle time via agent assist
- Multi-product support orgs needing smart routing

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | Custom | Solve + Triage + Assist bundle |

Pricing scales with ticket volume — expect five-figure annual minimum.

## Beginner verdict

Not a first AI tool. Right pick when you already have a helpdesk and want AI on top of it.
