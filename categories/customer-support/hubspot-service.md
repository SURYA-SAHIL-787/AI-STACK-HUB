## What it does

Free ticketing system tied to HubSpot CRM contacts. Breeze AI drafts replies, summarizes long ticket threads, and routes tickets by intent. If you already use HubSpot for sales, this slots in for free.

## Best use cases

- Small teams running HubSpot CRM + tickets in one place
- Free email-to-ticket pipeline
- AI-drafted replies from your existing help docs
- Migrating off shared Gmail inboxes

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Unlimited tickets, basic AI Breeze |
| Starter | $45/seat/mo | SLAs, routing, full AI |
| Pro | $90/seat/mo | Custom reports, automation |

## Beginner verdict

Best free pick if you're already in the HubSpot ecosystem.
