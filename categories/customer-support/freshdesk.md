## What it does

Freshdesk is a ticketing platform with Freddy AI for reply suggestions, ticket categorization, and an AI agent for self-service. Significantly cheaper than Zendesk at the low end, with a generous free tier for tiny teams.

## Best use cases

- Small teams that need ticketing + AI together
- Migrating off shared inboxes without big spend
- Multi-channel support (email, chat, social)
- B2C ops with predictable ticket types

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Up to 10 agents, basic ticketing |
| Growth | $15/agent/mo | Automations, basic Freddy |
| Pro | $49/agent/mo | Full Freddy AI, SLA mgmt |

## Beginner verdict

Best value paid pick. Free tier alone covers early-stage teams.
