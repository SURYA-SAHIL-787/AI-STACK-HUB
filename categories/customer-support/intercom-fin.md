## What it does

Fin is Intercom's AI agent — it reads your help docs, talks to customers, and resolves tickets without escalation. You pay per successful resolution, so cost scales with use. Industry-leading at deflection rates.

## Best use cases

- High-volume support teams cutting ticket load
- Replacing tier-1 support with AI
- Multilingual auto-replies (45+ languages)
- Pairing with a human team for tier-2 escalations

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Fin AI | $0.99/resolution | Pay only when Fin resolves a ticket |
| Intercom Essential | $39/seat/mo | Base inbox + Fin add-on |
| Advanced | $99/seat/mo | Workflows, copilot, full Fin |

## Beginner verdict

Most polished AI support agent today. Not for tiny teams — needs ticket volume to be worth it.
