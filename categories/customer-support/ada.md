## What it does

Ada is a no-code platform for building AI agents that handle support conversations across chat, email, voice, and social. Designed for non-technical CX teams to build and tune the AI without engineering help.

## Best use cases

- Enterprise CX teams replacing tier-1 fully
- Multilingual global support (50+ languages)
- Brands wanting non-engineers to own the bot
- Compliance-heavy industries (finance, healthcare)

Not for small teams. Strong enterprise pick when you outgrow Intercom/Zendesk's AI.
