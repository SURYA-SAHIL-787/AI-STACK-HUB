## What it does

ChatSpot by HubSpot lets you chat with your HubSpot CRM in natural language — "show me leads that haven't been contacted in 30 days", "draft an email to last quarter's customers", "summarize Acme Corp's history". Free with any HubSpot account.

## Best use cases

- HubSpot users wanting faster data access
- Reps avoiding clicking through HubSpot UI
- Generating quick reports
- Drafting outreach grounded in CRM data

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |

| Free | $0 | All features (HubSpot account required) |

## Beginner verdict

If you use HubSpot, install ChatSpot — free, useful, no downside.
