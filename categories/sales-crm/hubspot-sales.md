## What it does

Sales Hub adds to the free CRM: sequences (automated email follow-ups), playbooks, advanced AI (Breeze Copilot, Intelligence, Agents), reporting dashboards, custom properties, and removes free tier limits.

## Best use cases

- Teams running multi-step sales sequences
- Sales managers needing reports + forecasting
- Companies with structured pipelines

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $45/seat/mo (annual) | Full Sales Hub + AI |
| Professional | $90/seat/mo | Advanced automation |
| Enterprise | $150/seat/mo | Custom AI, SSO |

## Beginner verdict

Most-used paid CRM for SMBs. Worth it once you have ≥3 reps.
