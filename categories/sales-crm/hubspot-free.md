## What it does

Free forever CRM — unlimited contacts, deals, basic pipelines, email tracking. HubSpot Breeze AI is now integrated free: AI summarizes deals, drafts email replies, scores leads. Most-recommended free CRM.

## Best use cases

- Startups managing their first 100 customers
- Solo founders + freelancers
- Replacing spreadsheet-based contact tracking
- Free CRM with AI summaries built in

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |

Best free CRM in the world. Free tier alone covers most early-stage needs.
