## What it does

Attio is positioned as "what HubSpot would look like if built today" — Notion-like flexibility, AI enrichment (auto-fills companies/people from emails), AI workflows (trigger actions on events), and native AI Research (Attio searches the web for info on your contacts). Loved by YC-backed startups.

## Best use cases

- Startups outgrowing free HubSpot
- Tech teams wanting Notion-like CRM
- Replacing manual data entry with AI workflows
- Customer-facing teams that need automation

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Up to 3 users, basic AI |
| Plus | $29/user/mo | Workflows, advanced AI |
| Pro | $59/user/mo | More automation, integrations |
| Enterprise | Custom | SOC 2, SSO |

## Beginner verdict

Modern alternative to HubSpot. Free tier supports small teams. Try if HubSpot feels dated.
