## What it does

Folk is a modern relationship CRM with AI enrichment — paste a LinkedIn URL or email, Folk fills in name, role, company, contact details. Magic Fields auto-categorize contacts. Designed for people who hated traditional CRMs.

## Best use cases

- Solo founders tracking VCs, advisors, customers
- Agencies managing client + prospect lists
- Freelancers building referral networks
- Personal CRM for networking

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 1 workspace, basic features |
| Standard | $20/user/mo | Full AI enrichment, integrations |
| Premium | $40/user/mo | Advanced workflows |

## Beginner verdict

Best CRM for solo founders. Free tier is generous; Standard is reasonable when you scale.
