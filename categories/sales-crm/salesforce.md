## What it does

Einstein is Salesforce's AI suite — lead/opportunity scoring, AI forecasting, AI-generated email replies, Einstein GPT, and now Agentforce (autonomous AI agents that act in your CRM). Layered on top of Salesforce Sales Cloud / Service Cloud.

## Best use cases

- Mid-market / enterprise Salesforce shops
- Companies already paying for Salesforce
- Sales orgs wanting predictive forecasting
- Customer service teams deploying AI agents

## Pricing breakdown

| Aspect | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| Bundled | Higher Salesforce tier | Some included in top-tier SKUs |
