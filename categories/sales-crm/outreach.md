## What it does

Outreach is a sales engagement platform: manage cadences (multi-touch sequences across email/phone/LinkedIn), AI deal scoring, conversation intelligence (records calls + extracts insights), and AI follow-up suggestions. Used by major SaaS companies.

## Best use cases

- Mid-market+ outbound sales teams
- Sales reps with 100+ accounts each
- Companies replacing manual cadence management
- RevOps standardizing sales processes
