## What it does

Clay is a "spreadsheet on steroids" — build lead lists, enrich with data from 100+ sources (LinkedIn, Apollo, Clearbit, etc.), then use AI to personalize outreach at scale. Famous for "Clay GTM" plays that 10x outbound results.

## Best use cases

- Sales teams running personalized outbound at scale
- Growth marketers building lead lists
- RevOps automating data workflows
- Replacing 5 separate tools (enrichment + AI + CRM sync)

## Beginner verdict

Premium tool — only worth it once outbound is a real revenue lever. Not for first-time founders.
