## What it does

Apollo has a 270M+ contact database with AI-generated insights: who's hiring, who got funded, who fits your ICP. Free tier includes 10,000 contacts/mo + basic outreach sequences. Industry-standard outbound platform for startups.

## Best use cases

- B2B outbound sales prospecting
- Finding decision-makers at target accounts
- AI-written sequences for cold outreach
- Replacing manual LinkedIn searches

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 10k credits/mo, basic features |
| Basic | $49/user/mo | More credits + AI features |
| Professional | $79/user/mo | Advanced AI + sequences |
| Organization | $119/user/mo | Team + advanced |

## Beginner verdict

Best free tier for B2B outbound. Plenty for founders doing first-100 customer outreach.
