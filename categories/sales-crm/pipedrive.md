## What it does

Pipedrive is a pipeline-first CRM (drag deals across stages). AI assistant suggests next actions, summarizes deals, drafts emails. Simpler than HubSpot/Salesforce — chosen by SMBs that want no-fluff CRM.

## Best use cases

- Sales teams who think in pipelines
- SMBs not needing HubSpot's marketing features
- Replacing spreadsheet-based deal tracking
- Teams wanting affordable CRM + AI

## Beginner verdict

Cheapest serious paid CRM with AI. Good for teams that find HubSpot bloated.
