## What it does

Cleo connects to your bank account and talks to you like a friend — sometimes harshly — about your spending, savings goals, and budgets. The "roast me" mode went viral; under the hood it is a serious budgeting and credit-building tool aimed at Gen Z users.

## Best use cases

- First-time budgeting
- Building savings habits with goal nudges
- Curbing impulse spending
- Tracking subscriptions

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Chatbot, budgeting, basic insights |
| Plus / Builder | ~$6–15/month | Cash advances, credit-builder features |

## Beginner verdict

Lightweight, friendly, and actually fun — the personality lowers the barrier to engaging with money. Best for early-career users; not a wealth-management tool.
