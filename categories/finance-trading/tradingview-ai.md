## What it does

TradingView's AI layer overlays the world's most popular charting platform — auto-detecting patterns, summarizing indicators, and helping users write Pine Script for custom indicators and strategies. The base charting tool is free; AI extras are tied to higher tiers.

## Best use cases

- Spotting chart patterns (head and shoulders, flags, etc.)
- Explaining what an indicator says in plain language
- Writing or fixing Pine Script for backtests
- Multi-asset (stocks, crypto, forex) technical analysis

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Charts, basic AI helpers |
| Essential | ~$15/month | More indicators, ad-free |
| Plus | ~$25/month | More AI features |
| Premium | ~$60/month | Power-user toolkit |

## Beginner verdict

Best paired with learning technical analysis basics — the AI is most useful once you can already read a chart. Don't treat AI signals as trade recommendations.
