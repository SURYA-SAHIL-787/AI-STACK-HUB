## What it does

Trade Ideas runs market-wide scans for setups in real time and uses an AI engine ("Holly") to surface trade ideas with entry, stop, and target levels. Built for the day-trading workflow — fast scanning, alerts, and chart overlays.

## Best use cases

- Active intraday US equity trading
- Finding momentum setups across the whole market
- Pre-market gap and news scanning
- Swing-trade idea generation

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | ~$84/month | Core scanner |
| Premium | ~$167/month | Holly AI, advanced alerts |

## Beginner verdict

Not a beginner tool — built for traders who already manage risk and execute fast. The free trials are short; treat them as evaluation, not learning time.
