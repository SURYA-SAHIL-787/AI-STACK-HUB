## What it does

AlphaSense indexes millions of filings, earnings transcripts, broker research, expert-network interviews, and news — and layers AI search and summarization on top. After acquiring Tegus, it became the dominant tool for primary research at funds and corporates.

## Best use cases

- Buy-side research on a new company or sector
- Tracking themes across earnings calls
- Searching expert-network interviews for primary insight
- Corporate strategy and competitive intelligence

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | Custom (five+ figures/year) | Full platform, content licensing, team seats |

Pricing scales with content access (broker research, transcripts) and seat count.

## Beginner verdict

Not for individuals. If you join a research team that uses it, expect to live in it daily.
