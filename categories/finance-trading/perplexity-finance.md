## What it does

Perplexity's finance mode adds live tickers, charts, earnings summaries, and analyst takes to its standard answer-with-citations engine. Ask "what happened with NVDA today?" and you get a sourced explanation alongside the chart.

## Best use cases

- News-driven stock questions ("why is X down?")
- Earnings recaps in plain language
- Sector and macro overviews
- Comparing two companies side-by-side

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Full finance answers with citations |
| Pro | $20/month | Stronger models, more Pro searches |

## Beginner verdict

A great daily driver — citations make it much safer than ChatGPT for "what's the latest?" questions. Just remember it is research, not advice.

## Alternatives

- [ChatGPT](chatgpt-finance.md) — better for reasoning, weaker for live data
- [FinChat](finchat.md) — deeper fundamentals, less news-focused
