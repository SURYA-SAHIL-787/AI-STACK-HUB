## What it does

FinChat (now part of Stratosphere) is purpose-built for equity research — ask about a company's segments, KPIs, capex, or earnings-call themes and get structured answers with chart-ready data. Strong on US, Canadian, and European listings.

## Best use cases

- Comparing companies on specific KPIs
- Tracking segment revenue over time
- Earnings call summaries and Q&A search
- Building a watchlist with custom data points

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Limited daily questions, basic data |
| Plus | ~$29/month | Higher limits, full financial history |
| Pro | ~$79/month | Advanced charting and exports |

## Beginner verdict

Steeper learning curve than ChatGPT — but if you are doing real equity research, the structured data and citations save hours. Free tier is enough to evaluate.

## Alternatives

- [AlphaSense](alphasense.md) — institutional-grade alternative
- [Perplexity Finance](perplexity-finance.md) — better for news, weaker on data
