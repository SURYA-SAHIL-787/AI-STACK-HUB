## What it does

Composer lets you describe a strategy in plain English ("buy SPY when its 50-day moving average is above the 200-day") and turns it into an automated, backtested algorithm. Connect a brokerage account and the strategy runs without manual trades.

## Best use cases

- Systematic, rules-based ETF strategies
- Backtesting ideas before risking real money
- Removing emotion from trading
- Learning quantitative concepts hands-on

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Backtesting, view-only symphonies |
| Pro | ~$24/month | Live trading automation |

## Beginner verdict

A good way to learn systematic investing without writing Python — but understand the strategy before you fund it. Backtests are not future returns.
