## What it does

Bloomberg trained its own finance-domain LLM (BloombergGPT) and rolled AI summarization, news Q&A, and document analysis into the Terminal. The Terminal itself is the dominant institutional research platform; the AI layer accelerates how analysts use it.

## Best use cases

- Sell-side and buy-side research desks
- News and filing summarization at scale
- Cross-asset analytics for institutional traders
- Compliance-grade document workflows

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Terminal | ~$2,500/month/seat | Full Terminal + AI features |

Not bought by individuals — sold as multi-seat institutional licenses.

## Beginner verdict

Not for retail users. Knowing this exists is enough; if you ever join a finance desk, expect this to be on your screen.
