## What it does

ChatGPT is not a finance product, but it covers a huge amount of ground for free — explaining DCF, building Excel formulas, summarizing 10-Ks (with the paid tier's file uploads), and walking through tax or investing basics in plain language. Treat it as a tutor, not a data source.

## Best use cases

- Understanding financial concepts (DCF, IRR, options)
- Excel and Google Sheets formula help
- Drafting investment notes and summaries
- Building a financial vocabulary from scratch

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Daily message cap, GPT-4-class model |
| Plus | $20/month | Higher limits, file uploads, data analysis |
| Pro | $200/month | Power user features and reasoning models |

## Beginner verdict

The best starting point for anyone learning finance with AI. Just remember: ChatGPT can hallucinate numbers — verify any specific figure against a primary source.
