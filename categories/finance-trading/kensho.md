## What it does

Kensho is the AI engine behind many S&P Global products — entity linking, document classification, transcript analytics, and natural-language search across financial datasets. Most customers experience Kensho indirectly through S&P platforms (Capital IQ, Market Intelligence).

## Best use cases

- Linking news and events to securities at scale
- Earnings transcript analytics
- Document understanding for compliance and risk
- Powering downstream S&P Global workflows

## Beginner verdict

Not a tool an individual signs up for — more like infrastructure inside larger S&P products. Worth knowing as one of the longest-running AI players in finance (founded 2013).
