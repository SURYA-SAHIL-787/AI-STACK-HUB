## What it does

Copy.ai started as a generator for marketing copy (ads, emails, blog intros) and evolved into a "workflow" platform — chain prompts together to automate longer content tasks. Now positions itself as a "GTM AI Platform" for sales/marketing automation alongside writing.

## Best use cases

- Marketing copy at scale (ads, social posts, email subject lines)
- Building reusable content workflows
- Sales teams personalizing outreach
- Lower-cost alternative to Jasper

## Beginner verdict

Free tier is enough to evaluate. If you're not running a content team, ChatGPT Plus/Claude Pro will do similar work for less money.

- **Learning curve**: Moderate
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Pricing jumps fast from Starter to Advanced
