## Quick comparison

| Need | Best pick |
|---|---|
| Rewriting / paraphrasing | QuillBot |
| Grammar and spell check | Grammarly (free) / Grammarly Premium |
| Making writing more readable | Hemingway Editor |
| Marketing copy and blog posts | Jasper or Copy.ai |
| SEO-focused content | Writesonic |
| Fiction / novel writing | Sudowrite |
| Browser-wide writing help | HyperWrite |
| Free quick AI content drafts | Rytr |

*Note: For general drafting via chatbots, see [Chatbots & General AI](../chatbots-general-ai/README.md). Claude and ChatGPT often outperform dedicated writing tools for general drafting.*
