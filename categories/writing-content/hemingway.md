## What it does

Hemingway Editor color-codes your text to show: hard-to-read sentences (yellow), very hard sentences (red), passive voice (green), weak phrasing (purple), and complex words with simpler alternatives (blue). It scores your writing's grade level — the lower, the easier to read.

## Best use cases

- Editing long-form content (essays, blog posts, articles)
- Making technical writing more accessible
- Identifying sentences that need to be split
- Final pass before publishing

## Beginner verdict

The simplest, fastest way to make your writing clearer. No account needed — just paste text into the web editor. Use it on anything you write for an audience.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: None (web)
- **Gotchas**: Grading "simpler is better" can fight against academic/technical styles where complex sentences are appropriate
