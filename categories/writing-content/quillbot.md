## What it does

QuillBot started as a paraphrasing tool — paste text, get a rewritten version in different styles (Standard, Fluency, Formal, Creative, etc.). It now bundles grammar checker, summarizer, plagiarism checker, citation generator, and AI-content detector in one suite.

## Best use cases

- Rewriting essay sections to avoid repetition
- Translating awkward phrasing into smoother English (great for non-native writers)
- Quick grammar check before submitting
- Generating citations in APA/MLA format

## Beginner verdict

If you write academic content, papers, or anything that needs polish, QuillBot is the most accessible starter tool. Free tier is enough to evaluate before paying.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Paraphrased text can still be detected by AI/plagiarism detectors
