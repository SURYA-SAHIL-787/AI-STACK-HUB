## What it does

Writesonic specializes in SEO-optimized content generation — long-form articles with keyword integration, factual accuracy via web search, AI-generated images, and a built-in "Chatsonic" chatbot. Pricing is more accessible than Jasper/Copy.ai for individual creators.

## Best use cases

- SEO blog post generation
- Affiliate sites and content marketing
- Quick article drafts with built-in fact-checking
- Bloggers who don't need team features

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 (trial) | Limited credits to evaluate |
| Standard | $19/mo | 1 user, ~33k words/mo, all features |
| Professional | $49/mo | More credits, brand voice |
| Advanced | $99/mo | Even more credits, multiple users |

*Verify current pricing on the official website — Writesonic has rebranded tiers multiple times.*

## Beginner verdict

Good middle-ground between Rytr (cheap, limited) and Jasper (premium, expensive). Worth trying free trial if you're producing SEO content regularly.

- **Learning curve**: Moderate
- **Setup required**: Free trial account
