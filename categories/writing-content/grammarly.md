## What it does

Grammarly checks your writing for grammar, spelling, punctuation, and basic clarity as you type — anywhere on your computer. The free tier catches most common mistakes; Premium adds advanced suggestions for tone, conciseness, and engagement.

## Best use cases

- Catching typos and grammar mistakes before sending emails
- Polishing essays, reports, and assignments
- Real-time spell-check in any text field on your computer
- ESL writers wanting a second pair of eyes

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |

## Beginner verdict

Install the browser extension and forget about it — it'll quietly improve every email, post, and document you write. The free tier is enough for most people.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account + install browser extension
- **Gotchas**: Suggestions can be overaggressive — always review before accepting
