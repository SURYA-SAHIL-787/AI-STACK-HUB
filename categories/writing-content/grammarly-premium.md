## What it does

Grammarly Premium expands beyond grammar/spelling to suggest improvements in tone (match audience), clarity (cut wordiness), engagement (vary sentence rhythm), and delivery (politeness, formality). Also includes a plagiarism checker and AI-powered rewrite suggestions.

## Best use cases

- Professional email and report writing
- Academic papers (with plagiarism check)
- Job applications and cover letters
- Daily polishing of all written work

## Beginner verdict

If you write something graded or professional daily, $12/mo is reasonable. For casual users, free tier is plenty.

- **Learning curve**: Easy (same UX as free)
- **Setup required**: Upgrade existing Grammarly account
- **Gotchas**: Suggestions can be overaggressive — always review before accepting
