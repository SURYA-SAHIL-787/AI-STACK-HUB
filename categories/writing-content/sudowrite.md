## What it does

Sudowrite is purpose-built for creative fiction writing. Unique features include "Brainstorm" (generates ideas in your story's voice), "Describe" (adds sensory detail), "Twist" (suggests plot twists), "Canvas" (character/world-building boards), and "Story Bible" (track plot, character, lore across long works).

## Best use cases

- Drafting novels and long-form fiction
- Overcoming writer's block with brainstorming features
- Maintaining consistency across a long story (Story Bible)
- Screenwriting and serialized fiction

## Beginner verdict

If you're writing fiction seriously, Sudowrite's specialized features beat using a general chatbot. If you're casual or writing short stories, ChatGPT/Claude is fine.

- **Learning curve**: Moderate (many specialized features)
- **Setup required**: Paid account from day one (no free tier)
- **Gotchas**: AI fiction is controversial — check submission guidelines before submitting AI-assisted work to publishers

## Alternatives
