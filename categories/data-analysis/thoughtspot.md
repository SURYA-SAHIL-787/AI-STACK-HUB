## What it does

ThoughtSpot pioneered "search-based analytics" — analysts and business users ask questions in natural language against company data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, etc.) and get charts/answers. Sage is their AI layer that adds GPT-style conversational analytics on top.

## Best use cases

- Enterprises with central data warehouses
- Business analysts who want self-service data
- Replacing static dashboards with conversational analytics
- Large organizations where Tableau/Power BI feel rigid
