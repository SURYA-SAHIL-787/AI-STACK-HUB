## What it does

Akkio trains predictive ML models on your data without code — predict lead scores, customer churn, deal closing probability, ad performance. Upload data, pick what to predict, get a deployed model. Used heavily by sales/marketing teams to add predictive features to their workflows.

## Best use cases

- Lead scoring (which prospects will convert?)
- Customer churn prediction
- Forecasting sales/revenue
- A/B test analysis at scale

## Beginner verdict

Specialized tool — only relevant if you specifically need predictions on business data. For general analysis, Julius or ChatGPT are simpler.

- **Learning curve**: Moderate (ML concepts help)
