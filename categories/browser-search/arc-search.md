## What it does

Arc Search is a mobile browser with a built-in AI feature called "Browse for me." Type a query, and instead of showing search results, Arc visits the top sites and generates a synthesized answer page with sources. Like having Perplexity built into your browser address bar.

## Best use cases

- Mobile searches where ad-laden results frustrate you
- Quick fact-finding on the go
- Travel research (combines info from multiple sites)
- Hands-free shopping research
