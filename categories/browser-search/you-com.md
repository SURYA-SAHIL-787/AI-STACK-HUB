## What it does

You.com positioned itself early as an AI-first search engine — your queries return chat-style answers grounded in web results, with options to switch between models (GPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.). Includes "Agents" (custom AI workflows) and an "App Store" for niche AI tools.

## Best use cases

- Replacing default Google search with AI answers
- Quick model-comparison searches
- Developer-focused queries (code search built-in)
- Searches where you want grounded, cited answers

## Beginner verdict

A solid free Perplexity alternative. If you didn't click with Perplexity, try You.com.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Free tier model access is limited; Pro for serious use
