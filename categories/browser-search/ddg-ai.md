## What it does

DuckDuckGo AI Chat lets you chat with several AI models (GPT-4o-mini, Claude Haiku, Llama 3.3, Mistral) anonymously — no account, no chat history saved by DuckDuckGo, no training data shared with model providers. The simplest way to use multiple AI models privately.

## Best use cases

- Quick AI questions without committing to an account
- Privacy-conscious use (DDG doesn't log your chats)
- Comparing answers across multiple models quickly
- Backup when ChatGPT/Claude have outages

## Beginner verdict

If you want zero-commitment AI chat — no account, no history — this is the answer. For deeper conversations or document analysis, full ChatGPT/Claude accounts are better.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: None
- **Gotchas**: No conversation history (intentional — privacy feature)
