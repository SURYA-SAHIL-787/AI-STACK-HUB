## What it does

Microsoft 365 Copilot is the enterprise tier — AI integrated across Outlook (draft emails, summarize threads), Word (draft documents), Excel (analyze data, generate formulas), PowerPoint (create slides from prompts), Teams (meeting summaries, action items), and Edge (advanced sidebar with company data context). Pulls from your work files for grounded answers.

## Best use cases

- Knowledge workers in Microsoft 365 enterprises
- Replacing fragmented AI tools with one Microsoft-blessed suite
- IT-mandated AI for organizations
- Enterprises with sensitive data that can't go to external AI tools

## Beginner verdict

Not for individuals or students. Only worth it if your employer covers it AND you live in M365 apps all day.

- **Learning curve**: Easy (same UX as Office apps)
- **Setup required**: Existing M365 subscription + Copilot add-on
- **Gotchas**: $30/user/mo adds up fast for teams — pilot before rolling out
