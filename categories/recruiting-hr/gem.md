---

## What it does

Gem's free Chrome extension surfaces candidate emails as you browse LinkedIn. Limited free credits per month. The paid product is a full recruiting CRM with AI outreach sequences.

## Best use cases

- Pulling emails from LinkedIn profiles
- Solo sourcing without paying for a full ATS
- Test-driving sourcing before buying a paid plan
- Agency sourcers tracking outreach

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free extension | $0 | Limited free email lookups/mo |
| Paid CRM | $100+/user/mo | Full sequences, analytics, AI |

## Beginner verdict

Best free sourcing tool to install today. The CRM upgrade is for serious teams only.

## Alternatives

- [SeekOut](seekout.md) — broader talent search
- [Manatal](manatal.md) — full ATS instead of just sourcing
- LinkedIn Recruiter Lite — paid LinkedIn-only alternative
