## What it does

Manatal is an ATS with AI candidate matching, social media enrichment, and a Chrome extension for sourcing. Significantly cheaper than the big ATS platforms — popular with small agencies in APAC and emerging markets.

## Best use cases

- Small recruiting agencies needing affordable ATS
- Startups with their first 50 hires
- Multi-client recruiting setups
- AI candidate scoring on inbound applicants

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free trial | $0 | 14 days full access |
| Professional | $15/user/mo | Core ATS + AI |
| Enterprise | $35/user/mo | Custom workflows |

## Beginner verdict

Best budget ATS with real AI features. Free trial is enough to evaluate fully.
