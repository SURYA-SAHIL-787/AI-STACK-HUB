## What it does

Phenom's "Intelligent Talent Experience" connects candidate sourcing, career sites, internal mobility, and recruiter copilots under one AI. Strong on candidate experience — personalized career sites, AI job match.

## Best use cases

- Modernizing a clunky career site with AI matching
- Enterprise internal mobility programs
- Recruiter copilot for high-volume teams
- Multi-region, multi-language candidate experiences

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |

| Enterprise | Custom | Full talent experience platform |

Quote-based, typically six-figure annual.

## Beginner verdict

Not for SMBs. Strong choice when modernizing a Fortune 500 career site + ATS.
