## What it does

iCIMS is one of the oldest enterprise ATS platforms — now bundles AI for resume screening, candidate matching, and chatbot screening. Default pick for Fortune 1000 talent acquisition teams.

## Best use cases

- Fortune 1000 talent acquisition at scale
- Compliance-heavy hiring (regulated industries)
- Multi-country, multi-language hiring
- ATS migrations from legacy systems
