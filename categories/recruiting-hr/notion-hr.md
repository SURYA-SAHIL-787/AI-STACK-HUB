## What it does

Notion's AI lives inside your Notion workspace. For HR teams, it drafts employee handbooks, policy docs, JDs, onboarding checklists, and performance review templates. Free trial of AI; paid tier bundles it into the Plus plan.

## Best use cases

- Building an employee handbook from scratch
- Drafting onboarding docs for new hires
- Summarizing policy threads from Slack
- Performance review templates with AI prompts
