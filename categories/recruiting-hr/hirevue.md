## What it does

HireVue runs async video interviews — candidates record answers on their own time, AI surfaces signals (no longer scores faces; that practice was retired). Used by Goldman Sachs, Unilever, and others for volume hiring.

## Best use cases

- Campus recruiting (thousands of grad apps)
- Contact center and retail volume hiring
- First-round screening at scale
- Game-based assessments for entry-level roles

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Essentials | ~$35k/yr | Core video + assessments |
| Enterprise | Custom | Advanced AI insights, ATS integration |

## Beginner verdict

Right for high-volume hiring only. Doesn't fit teams making <50 hires/yr.
