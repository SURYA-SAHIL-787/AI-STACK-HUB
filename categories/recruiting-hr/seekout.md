## What it does

SeekOut indexes 800M+ candidate profiles across LinkedIn, GitHub, patents, and publications. AI search lets you query naturally ("senior Rust engineer in Berlin"). Strong for diversity sourcing filters. Free trial available.

## Best use cases

- Hiring deep-tech engineers (GitHub data)
- Diversity sourcing with bias-aware filters
- Executive search across niches
- Talent mapping a competitor's org

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free trial | $0 | Limited searches |
| Standard | ~$8k/seat/yr | Full platform |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom workflows |

## Beginner verdict

Free trial is worth running for any serious sourcing role. Paid is expensive but accurate.
