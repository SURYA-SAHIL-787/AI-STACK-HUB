## What it does

Not a recruiting tool, but the most-used AI assistant for recruiters. Drafts job descriptions, generates screening questions, writes outreach messages, and polishes offer letters. Free tier handles most use.

## Best use cases

- Writing a JD from a 3-bullet brief
- Generating 10 screening questions per role
- Personalizing cold outreach to candidates
- Polishing offer letters or rejection emails

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | GPT-5 mini, basic features |
| Plus | $20/mo | GPT-5, faster responses |
| Team | $25/user/mo | Shared workspace |

## Beginner verdict

Every recruiter should use it for first drafts. Free tier is enough.
