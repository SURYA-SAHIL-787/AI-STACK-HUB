## What it does

Eightfold is a Talent Intelligence Platform — AI-powered candidate matching, internal mobility, and workforce planning. Used by large enterprises to unify recruiting + HR + L&D under one AI model.

## Best use cases

- Enterprise replacing fragmented ATS + HRIS + LMS
- Internal mobility programs at scale
- Diversity hiring with bias controls
- Reskilling existing workforce based on AI gap analysis

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise | Custom | Full platform, dedicated CSM |

Typically six-figure annual contracts, scoped to headcount.

## Beginner verdict

Not for SMBs. The right pick if you're a Fortune 500 modernizing talent ops.
