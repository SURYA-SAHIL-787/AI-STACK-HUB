## What it does

Olivia is Paradox's AI recruiter — handles candidate questions, prescreens, and books interviews via text/chat without human input. Used heavily in QSR (McDonald's), retail, and healthcare frontline roles.

## Best use cases

- Quick-serve restaurant hiring (one-day apply-to-hire)
- Retail and hospitality volume hiring
- Reducing candidate drop-off from slow scheduling
- Multilingual frontline hiring (SMS-based)
