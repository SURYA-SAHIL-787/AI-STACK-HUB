## What it does

Spike reformats emails as chat conversations (sender bubbles, no quoted headers). Adds AI features — Magic AI to draft replies, summarize threads, translate, and rewrite tone. Works with any IMAP email account.

## Best use cases

- Teams that wish email worked like Slack
- Reading long threads without scrolling through quoted history
- Quick AI drafting + tone adjustments
- Multi-account inbox management

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Basic Spike + limited AI |
| Pro | $5/user/mo | Unlimited AI, priority inbox, integrations |

## Beginner verdict

Best alternative client if email-as-chat appeals to you. Free tier is enough to try.
