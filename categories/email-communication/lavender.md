## What it does

Lavender installs in your email client and gives every draft a "Lavender Score" — likelihood of getting a reply based on length, tone, formatting, personalization, mobile-readability. Suggests specific improvements. Also includes prospect research (pulls LinkedIn data into the email panel) and AI-generated outreach drafts.

## Best use cases

- Sales reps trying to improve cold email reply rates
- BDRs scaling personalized outreach
- Founders doing investor or partnership emails
- Marketing teams doing outbound campaigns

## Beginner verdict

Not for casual email — purely a sales productivity tool. If outbound is your job, $29 pays off in one extra meeting.
