## What it does

Smart Compose suggests completions as you type. Help Me Write generates full email drafts from short prompts. Polish makes existing drafts more professional. Auto-summary on long threads. Most features free; the more powerful Gemini features inside Gmail come with Google AI Pro.

## Best use cases

- Replying to routine emails 2x faster
- Drafting outreach from short prompts
- Summarizing long forwarded threads
- Polishing tone before sending

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Gmail Free | $0 | Smart Compose, basic AI suggestions |
| Google AI Pro | $20/mo | Gemini-powered Help Me Write, advanced features, plus broader AI Pro bundle |

## Beginner verdict

The default starting point — free, built-in, no extra signup. For heavier AI use inside Gmail, Google AI Pro bundles it with Drive + NotebookLM.
