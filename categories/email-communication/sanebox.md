## What it does

SaneBox doesn't replace your email client — it adds AI sorting on top. Learns which senders matter to you and auto-moves unimportant email to a SaneLater folder. Includes SaneRemindMe (follow-up reminders), SaneBlackHole (auto-block), and digest summaries.

## Best use cases

- Inbox bankruptcy survivors
- Frequent flyers / travelers (digest summary while away)
- Anyone receiving 100+ emails/day
- People who want filtering without switching email clients

Cheapest serious inbox-AI tool. Works with any email — Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, etc. Free trial is the easy first step.
