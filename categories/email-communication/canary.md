## What it does

Canary positions privacy first — Sidekick AI processes locally where possible, supports PGP end-to-end encryption, and has explicit privacy policies. AI features include reply drafting, summary, smart sort, and follow-up reminders.

## Best use cases

- Journalists, lawyers, doctors handling sensitive email
- Anyone uncomfortable with Big Tech email AI
- PGP-encrypted email workflows
- Privacy-first AI assistant in email

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Basic Canary + limited AI |
| Pro | $20/year (~$1.67/mo) | Unlimited AI, all features, multi-account |

## Beginner verdict

Cheapest paid email AI option ($20/yr). Worth it if privacy matters in your workflow.
