## What it does

Mailbutler adds AI features to email clients you already use. AI Smart Compose, AI Summarize Threads, AI Smart Search, plus task extraction (turn email into tasks), tracking (read receipts), follow-up reminders, and signatures.

## Best use cases

- Apple Mail users (only AI option for native Mac Mail)
- Outlook users wanting better AI than M365 Copilot Excel-style integration
- Power users wanting tasks + tracking + AI in one tool
- Multiple email accounts across providers

## Beginner verdict

Specifically useful for Apple Mail users since few other AI tools work there. Otherwise, Shortwave Pro / Spark Premium offer similar features cheaper.
