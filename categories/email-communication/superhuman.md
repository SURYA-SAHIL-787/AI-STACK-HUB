## What it does

Superhuman is the premium email client — keyboard shortcuts for everything, snippets, scheduled send, follow-up reminders, AI write/summarize/triage, and "Hyper-personalization" for outreach. Famous for personalized onboarding sessions for new users. Recently added Superhuman AI for autonomous email handling.

## Best use cases

- Inbox-heavy roles (founders, sales, executives)
- People who type fast and want keyboard-everything
- Premium feel + concierge support
- Replacing email-management productivity apps

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $30/mo (annual) | All features for individuals |
| Business | $40/user/mo | Team features, admin |

## Beginner verdict

Only worth it if email is your job. For most people, free Gmail AI + maybe Shortwave Pro covers the use case for much less.

## Alternatives

- [Shortwave Pro](shortwave.md) — cheaper AI-first alternative
- [Spark Premium](spark.md) — much cheaper Smart Inbox approach
