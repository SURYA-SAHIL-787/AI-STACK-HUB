## What it does

Notion Mail is the newest entry — bundles email, calendar, and Notion docs in one workspace. AI features include auto-categorization, smart reply, AI search across emails, and inline drafting with Notion AI. Currently Gmail-only on the backend.

## Best use cases

- Heavy Notion users wanting AI email in one tool
- Anyone tired of switching between email, calendar, and docs
- Mac users (rollout started there)
