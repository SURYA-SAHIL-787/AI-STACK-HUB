## What it does

Spark introduced "Smart Inbox" years ago (groups newsletters, notifications, personal). Now adds AI write (drafts from short prompts), AI summary (long threads → bullets), AI rewrite (tone, length). Multi-account, free tier covers most personal use.

## Best use cases

- Personal inbox that's drowning in newsletters
- Quick drafts on mobile
- Summarizing long forwarded threads
- Multiple email accounts in one app

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Smart Inbox, basic AI, multi-account |
| Premium | $4.99/mo | Unlimited AI, team collaboration, advanced |
| Teams | $8.99/user/mo | Shared drafts, team inboxes |

## Beginner verdict

If Gmail's AI feels limited and you want a smarter client, Spark Free is the easiest switch. Mac/iOS users especially.
