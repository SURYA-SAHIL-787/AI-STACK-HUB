## What it does

Built by ex-Google Inbox team after Google killed Inbox. Sits on top of Gmail with AI as a first-class citizen — AI assistant that drafts replies, summarizes threads, searches naturally ("show me invoices from last month"), and bundles related emails. Pro tier removes limits + adds advanced features.

## Best use cases

- Gmail users who miss Google Inbox
- Heavy email users wanting AI without paying Superhuman prices
- Founders/PMs who need fast inbox triage
- Anyone wanting Inbox-style bundles back

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Basic Shortwave + limited AI |
| Pro | $9/mo (annual) or $12/mo (monthly) | Unlimited AI, advanced features |
| Business | Custom | Team features, admin |

## Beginner verdict

Best mid-priced AI email client. Try the free tier; Pro at $9 is the sweet spot vs Superhuman's $30.
