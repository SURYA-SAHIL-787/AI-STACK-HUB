## What it does

Rezi Pro lifts the free tier's "1 resume" cap, unlocks unlimited AI bullet/summary generation, gives you all templates, and includes Rezi's AI Cover Letter writer. The Lifetime option ($129 one-time) is rare in this category and works out cheaper for anyone who'll job-hunt more than once.

## Best use cases

- Maintaining multiple tailored resumes (one per role type)
- Iterating on AI-generated bullets for the perfect framing
- Anyone planning to use AI resume tools across multiple job searches over years (Lifetime tier)
- Heavy placement season application work

## Beginner verdict

For students entering placements, Pro Monthly ($16 for 1–2 months) covers the placement window. Lifetime is worth it only if you commit to using Rezi over many years.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from free Rezi account
- **Gotchas**: Lifetime tier is tied to the company existing — be aware of small-vendor risk
