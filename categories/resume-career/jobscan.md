## What it does

Jobscan is the most comprehensive ATS analysis tool. Paste your resume + a job description and it gives you a match score, lists missing keywords (skills, job titles, qualifications), checks ATS-readability (formatting issues), and offers LinkedIn profile optimization. Used by recruiters and career coaches.

## Best use cases

- Applying to a small number of high-value roles (top companies, executive positions)
- Optimizing LinkedIn profile for recruiter discovery
- Career coaching practices
- Final pre-submission check on critical applications

## Beginner verdict

Most expensive option here, justified only for high-stakes applications. Free 5 scans/month is enough for most placement candidates — use it to spot-check important applications.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Don't pay monthly unless heavily applying — use free scans strategically
