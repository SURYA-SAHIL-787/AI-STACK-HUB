## What it does

Enhancv Pro unlocks unlimited resumes, removes the watermark on exports, gives full access to premium templates (including LinkedIn-style and tech-specific designs), unlimited AI rewrites that quantify your impact, and ATS-compatible export options.

## Best use cases

- Mid-career tech professionals with quantifiable accomplishments
- Consulting and product roles where impact framing matters
- Candidates competitive with top-company applicants
- Maintaining multiple tailored versions for different role types

## Beginner verdict

Higher price than Teal+ or Rezi Pro. Justified if you're in a competitive senior search; overkill for first placements where ATS optimization matters more than design.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from free Enhancv account
- **Gotchas**: 3-Month tier is the sweet spot; monthly is poor value
