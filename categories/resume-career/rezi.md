## What it does

Rezi is laser-focused on ATS (Applicant Tracking System) compatibility — the software 90%+ of large employers use to filter resumes before humans see them. AI writes ATS-friendly bullet points, suggests keywords from job descriptions, and scores your resume against ATS criteria.

## Best use cases

- Applying to large companies that filter via ATS (most placement employers)
- Optimizing keywords for specific job descriptions
- Recent graduates without much "writing the perfect bullet" experience
- Career changers who need help phrasing transferable skills

## Beginner verdict

Best for placement applicants applying to large companies (TCS, Infosys, Accenture, banks, etc.) — they all use ATS. Free tier is enough to build one strong resume.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Plain template aesthetic; not impressive for design-focused roles
