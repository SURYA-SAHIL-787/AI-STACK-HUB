## What it does

Teal combines several job-hunt tools into one platform: a resume builder, an AI "Match Score" that compares your resume against job descriptions, a Chrome extension that saves job postings as you browse, and a Kanban-style application tracker. The free tier covers everything a job-hunter needs to evaluate the platform.

## Best use cases

- Tracking 20+ job applications across multiple companies
- Tailoring your resume to specific job descriptions (Match Score)
- Saving jobs from LinkedIn/Indeed/company sites in one place
- Students starting placement season

## Beginner verdict

The single best free tool for active job hunting. If you're entering placement season, install the Chrome extension and start tracking from day one.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account + Chrome extension
- **Gotchas**: Free Match Score has some daily limits; Teal+ unlocks unlimited
