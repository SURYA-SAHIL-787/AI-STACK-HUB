## What it does

Enhancv pushes you toward modern resume design + impact-focused content. AI suggests rewrites that quantify achievements ("increased X by Y%"), adds visual sections beyond standard work history (strengths, "my time" pie charts, etc.), and offers ATS-compatible variants. Heavily used by tech and consulting candidates.

## Best use cases

- Mid-career professionals reframing experience for new roles
- Tech, consulting, and product roles where impact metrics matter
- Replacing dull resumes with modern-but-readable formats
- Practicing "impact" framing for interviews too

## Beginner verdict

For first jobs / placements, simpler tools (Rezi, Teal) cover the basics. Enhancv shines once you have real experience to frame.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Watermarked free exports; some recruiters in conservative industries prefer plain formats
