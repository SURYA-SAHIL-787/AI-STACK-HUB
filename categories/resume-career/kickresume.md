## What it does

Kickresume offers visually-designed resume and cover letter templates (more design-forward than Rezi or Teal), with an AI writer that drafts bullet points. Includes a personal website builder that turns your resume into an online portfolio. Free tier is limited but produces a solid first resume.

## Best use cases

- Design, marketing, or creative roles where visual polish matters
- Building a personal portfolio website from your resume
- Wanting your resume to stand out aesthetically
- Cover letter generation alongside the resume

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |

## Beginner verdict

If aesthetics matter (creative roles), Kickresume wins on templates. For ATS-heavy applications, Rezi or Teal are better. Many users use Kickresume for design and Rezi for content.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Free tier exports are watermarked — Premium needed for real submissions

## Alternatives
