## What it does

Resume.com is a no-frills resume builder owned by Indeed. Everything is free — templates, AI writing assist, PDF download, no watermarks, no "upgrade for full export" tricks. Quality is basic but functional.

## Best use cases

- First-ever resume (high school, early college)
- One-off job application where you need a quick resume
- Students with zero budget
- Backup resume if other tools have export issues

## Ratings from around the web

- **Reddit sentiment**: Positive for genuine free-ness; criticized for plain templates and limited AI
- **Common feedback**: "Use this if you can't pay; everything else has gotchas"

*Ratings as of 2026-05. Re-verify periodically.*

## Beginner verdict

If you need a resume RIGHT NOW with zero friction and zero cost, this is the answer. For ongoing job-hunt workflows, use Teal or Rezi instead.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Free account (Indeed account works)
- **Gotchas**: Limited customization vs paid alternatives
