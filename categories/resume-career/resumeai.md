## What it does

ResumeAI is an AI resume builder and ATS checker at withresumeai.com. Free ATS checks: **3/day with no account**, **10/day with a free account**. It also publishes the open **State of ATS 2026** dataset — **738 large employers**, **704 portal-verified**, with a verified Workday share of **37.9%** (not the retracted 75.4% figure). A live job-seeker leaderboard offers **paid placement/visibility** (not pay-for-score, not pay-to-get-hired).

## Best use cases

- Free ATS readability / keyword checks before applying
- Understanding which ATS platforms large employers actually use
- Building or iterating an ATS-friendly resume
- Optional leaderboard visibility for job seekers

## Beginner verdict

Strong free ATS path plus uniquely useful open ATS market data. Leaderboard is optional paid visibility — Resume Score does not determine paid rank.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Optional free account
- **Gotchas**: Domain is withresumeai.com (not resume.ai)
