# ResumeAI

**URL:** https://withresumeai.com/

**Pricing:** Free tier (3 ATS checks/day anonymous; 10/day with free account)

**What it does:** Free ATS resume checker plus the open State of ATS 2026 dataset (738 employers, 704 portal-verified; Workday 37.9%).

**Best for:** Candidates who want a free ATS scan without a paid Jobscan-style plan.
