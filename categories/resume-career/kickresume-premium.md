## What it does

Kickresume Premium removes watermarks, unlocks all templates and designs (~30+), enables the AI writer for unlimited bullet generation, and adds the personal website builder — turning your resume into an online portfolio at a yourname.kickresume.com URL.

## Best use cases

- Design/creative/marketing roles needing visually striking resumes
- Building a quick personal portfolio website without web-dev work
- Annual subscribers (huge discount vs monthly)
- Anyone for whom resume aesthetic matters more than ATS score

## Beginner verdict

If designs matter for your roles, Kickresume Annual is reasonable. For technical roles where ATS matters most, Rezi or Teal are better picks.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from free Kickresume account
- **Gotchas**: Don't buy monthly — annual is 60% cheaper
