## What it does

Teal+ removes free-tier limits on AI generations, gives you advanced Match Score (per-bullet ATS scoring), unlocks all premium templates, AI cover letter generation, and priority support. The "$9/mo weekly" tier is unusual — designed for short active job-search bursts.

## Best use cases

- Active placement season (1–3 months of heavy applying)
- Switching jobs while employed (compressed time)
- Tailoring 10+ applications per week
- Students applying to 50+ companies during placements
