## What it does

Hugging Face is the largest hub of open-source AI models — LLMs (Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek), vision models, audio models, embedding models, and more. Their Inference API gives you free hosted access to many models with rate-limited free tier. Inference Endpoints add managed paid hosting for production.

## Best use cases

- Trying many open-source models without setup
- ML researchers and students learning model architectures
- Building apps with specialized models (translation, sentiment, etc.)
- Self-hosting comparison before committing to specific models

## Beginner verdict

Worth a free account just for the model browsing experience. Production use requires Inference Endpoints — for student/hobby projects, Groq or Together AI are simpler.

- **Learning curve**: Moderate to Steep
- **Setup required**: Free account + API token
- **Gotchas**: Free tier latency can be slow; dedicated endpoints get expensive
