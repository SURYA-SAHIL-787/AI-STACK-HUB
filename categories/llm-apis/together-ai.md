## What it does

Together AI hosts open-source LLMs (Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek, Qwen, and many more) on optimized infrastructure. Free credits on signup let you evaluate; production use is pay-per-token at prices typically lower than going through OpenAI/Anthropic. Includes fine-tuning, dedicated endpoints, and inference clustering.

## Best use cases

- Production apps on Llama/Mistral/DeepSeek
- Fine-tuning open models on custom data
- Cost-conscious deployment at scale
- Developers committed to open-source AI stack

## Beginner verdict

After Groq for prototyping, Together AI is a natural production path for open-source models. For frontier-model quality, OpenAI/Anthropic APIs are needed.

- **Learning curve**: Moderate (OpenAI-compatible API)
- **Setup required**: Free account + API key
- **Gotchas**: Some models have rate limits even on paid; check current state per model
