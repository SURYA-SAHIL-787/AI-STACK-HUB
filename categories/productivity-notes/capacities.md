## What it does

Capacities is built around "objects" — instead of just pages, you create typed entities (People, Books, Movies, Ideas, Projects) that connect to each other through links and properties. Includes an AI assistant for summarization, drafting, and finding connections. Templates let you start fast.

## Best use cases

- Building a knowledge base where things are entities (a book has an author, who wrote other books)
- Tracking personal learning across many sources
- Students managing research with structured entities
- Replacing Notion if you find pages-and-databases too generic

## Beginner verdict

Worth exploring if you've outgrown Notion's free-form pages. The object-based approach is unusual but rewarding for the right user. If you mainly take linear notes, Notion or Obsidian is simpler.

- **Learning curve**: Moderate
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: 1,000-block free tier hits fast on active use; Pro is required for serious work

## Alternatives

- [Notion (Free)](notion.md) — more popular, less structured
