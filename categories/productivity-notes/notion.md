## What it does

Notion combines documents, databases, wikis, and project management into one tool. You can build dashboards, course notes, project trackers, personal CRMs, and habit trackers — all using "blocks" you arrange like Lego. AI features are sold separately as Notion AI.

## Best use cases

- Student note-taking and class organization
- Personal knowledge management (the "second brain" use case)
- Project tracking and task management
- Building team wikis and documentation

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|

## Beginner verdict

The single most-recommended productivity tool for students. Free tier covers everything you need for personal use. Don't add Notion AI until you've used Notion for a few weeks first.

- **Learning curve**: Moderate (lots of possibilities)
- **Setup required**: Free account
- **Gotchas**: Can become a tinkering hole — set up basic workflows, then USE them
