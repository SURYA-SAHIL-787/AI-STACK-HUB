## What it does

Notion AI adds AI features inside your Notion workspace. You can ask it questions about your notes ("what did I write about that interview?"), have it write/edit/summarize, auto-fill database properties (extract names from meeting notes, tag tasks, generate translations), and search across all your pages and databases conversationally.

## Best use cases

- Heavy Notion users who want AI without switching tools
- Meeting notes → action items auto-extraction
- Asking questions across years of notes
- Teams that have built workflows in Notion

## Beginner verdict

Don't add until you've used Notion (the base tool) for a few weeks and have a real workspace built up. Notion AI shines when there's content to chat with.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Add to existing Notion account
- **Gotchas**: Quality is good but not market-leading; Claude/ChatGPT often produce better writing
