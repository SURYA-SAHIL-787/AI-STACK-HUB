## What it does

Reflect is a notes app structured around daily notes (think Roam/Logseq, simpler). Built-in AI (powered by GPT-4) helps you summarize, transform, and brainstorm without leaving the app. Features include backlinks, hashtags, daily notes auto-creation, end-to-end encryption, and AI commands you can run on any block.

## Best use cases

- Daily journaling with AI reflection prompts
- Knowledge workers who think by writing every day
- Writers using notes as a thinking tool
- Privacy-conscious users (E2E encrypted by default)
