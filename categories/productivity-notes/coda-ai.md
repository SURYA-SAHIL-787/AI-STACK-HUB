## What it does

Coda is a document tool where every doc can include tables that act like databases — you can build trackers, dashboards, and lightweight apps inside a single doc. Coda AI adds in-doc AI writing, table generation, summaries, and a "AI Assistant" that can answer questions and automate workflows across your docs.

## Best use cases

- Project leads tracking work in a doc-database hybrid
- Building team dashboards with AI-generated summaries
- Replacing Notion for users who need stronger table/automation features
- Lightweight CRMs and trackers with AI auto-fill

## Beginner verdict

If you need spreadsheet-like power inside your docs, Coda + Coda AI is a strong fit. For simple notes, Notion is the easier path.

- **Learning curve**: Moderate
- **Setup required**: Free account → upgrade for AI
- **Gotchas**: Maker-based pricing is different from per-user — read carefully
