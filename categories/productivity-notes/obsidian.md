## What it does

Obsidian stores all your notes as plain markdown files on your own computer. The killer feature is bi-directional linking — when you mention `[[topic]]` in one note, it auto-connects to a topic page that shows everywhere you've referenced it. Plugins extend it into a Notion-like tool: AI chat with your notes (Copilot for Obsidian, Smart Connections), kanban boards, mind maps, daily journals.

## Best use cases

- "Second brain" personal knowledge management
- Note-taking that survives any company shutting down (it's just markdown files)
- AI chat with your own notes via plugins
- Researchers / students who own their data

## Beginner verdict

Not the easiest first tool — but the right tool if you value owning your data and want unlimited customization. Pair with Smart Connections or Copilot for Obsidian (free plugins) for AI features.

- **Learning curve**: Moderate to steep
- **Setup required**: Install desktop/mobile app, configure your "vault" folder
- **Gotchas**: Free sync requires using iCloud/Dropbox; Obsidian Sync is paid
