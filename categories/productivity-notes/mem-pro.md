## What it does

Mem Pro unlocks the full AI behind Mem — unlimited Mem Chat (chat with all your notes), auto-tagging, meeting transcription, advanced search across your entire notebook, integrations (calendar, email, etc.), and unlimited notes. The free trial gives a taste; Pro is required for real use.

## Best use cases

- Daily note-takers who want AI to do the organization
- Meeting-heavy professionals (Mem captures and links meeting notes well)
- Replacing several apps (Notion + Otter + ChatGPT) with one
- People with thousands of legacy notes they want indexed

## Beginner verdict

Higher price than alternatives, justified only if you're going to feed notes into it daily. Try the free trial first — if you're not adding 5+ notes/day, skip.

- **Learning curve**: Easy
- **Setup required**: Upgrade from trial
- **Gotchas**: Long-term company viability questions — back up your notes regularly
