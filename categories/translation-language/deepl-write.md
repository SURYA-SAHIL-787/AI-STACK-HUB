## What it does

DeepL Write is a Grammarly-like tool focused on non-native writers. Improves grammar, vocabulary, tone, and clarity in English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch. Often catches nuances Grammarly misses for non-native English users.

## Best use cases

- Non-native English speakers polishing professional writing
- Multilingual writers working across 2-3 languages
- Refining DeepL translations to sound more natural
- Replacing Grammarly Premium with the DeepL bundle

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Basic Write features, character limits |
| Pro | Bundled with DeepL Pro ($8.74+/mo) | Unlimited, full features |

## Beginner verdict

Particularly useful for Indian students writing English for placements — catches phrasing patterns Grammarly misses. Bundled free with DeepL Pro.
