## What it does

Google Translate is the universal free translator. 130+ languages (more than DeepL), camera translation (point phone at sign), conversation mode (real-time bilingual), voice input, website translation. Quality varies by language pair — strong for major languages, weaker for less-common ones (though steadily improving with Google's AI investments).

## Best use cases

- Travel (camera translation, offline mode)
- Less common languages (Tamil, Vietnamese, Swahili, etc.)
- Quick word-by-word lookups
- Translating entire websites with one click

## Beginner verdict

Installed on every phone for a reason. Use for casual + travel; DeepL for quality-sensitive translation.
