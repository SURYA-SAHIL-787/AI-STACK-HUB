## What it does

Lingvanex offers translation with stronger privacy guarantees than Google/DeepL — on-device translation in their desktop/mobile apps, no logging on paid tiers, and self-hosted API for enterprise. Supports 100+ languages. Lower brand awareness than competitors but solid quality.

## Best use cases

- Translating sensitive content (legal, medical)
- Companies that can't send text to Google/DeepL
- Offline translation while traveling
- Self-hosted translation API for apps

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Limited daily characters |
| Personal Pro | $9.99/mo | Unlimited, all features |
| Business / API | Custom | Self-hosted, SLA, enterprise |

## Beginner verdict

Niche but useful. For most personal use, DeepL Free covers you. Lingvanex matters when privacy is non-negotiable.

## Alternatives

- [DeepL](deepl.md) — higher quality, less privacy-focused
- [Google Translate](google-translate.md) — most features, least privacy
- Apple Translate (iOS, on-device, free)
