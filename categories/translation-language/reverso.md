## What it does

Reverso translates with context — shows your translated phrase used in real example sentences from books, films, news, and the web. Helps you learn how phrases are actually used. Also includes grammar checker, conjugation, synonyms, and "Reverso Documents" for translating Word/PDF files.

## Best use cases

- Learning a foreign language (see real-world usage)
- Writers translating with cultural nuance
- Verifying that an awkward translation is actually correct
- Conjugation lookups for complex verb forms

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Translation, examples, conjugation; with ads |
| Premium | ~$9.99/mo | Ad-free, unlimited document translation, longer texts |

## Beginner verdict

Free version is fantastic for learners. Premium only worth it if you translate documents regularly.

## Alternatives

- [DeepL](deepl.md) — purer translation focus
- [Google Translate](google-translate.md) — broader coverage
- Linguee (now owned by DeepL) — also context-focused
