## What it does

Crowdin competes with Lokalise — translation management with AI-assisted translation, integrations (GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, Figma, design tools), community-translation workflows (popular with open-source projects), and machine translation engines plugged in (DeepL, Google, custom).

## Best use cases

- Open-source projects accepting community translations
- Indie game devs localizing to multiple languages
- Small SaaS teams managing translation budgets
- Mobile app localization

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free (open-source) | $0 | For verified open-source projects |
| Pro | $50/mo | 1 project, basic AI |
| Team | $200/mo | More projects, MT engines |
| Business | Custom | Advanced features |

## Beginner verdict

For indie devs and OSS maintainers, Crowdin's open-source tier is fantastic. For commercial product teams, Lokalise has more mindshare in 2026.
