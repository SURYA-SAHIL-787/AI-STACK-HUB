## What it does

Frontier LLMs handle translation surprisingly well — and unlike DeepL/Google, you can give context ("translate this for a formal business email to a Japanese client" or "make this sound natural in Mexican Spanish, not Spain Spanish"). Less consistent than purpose-built translators for raw quality but unmatched for nuance.

## Best use cases

- Adapting tone for specific audiences
- Translating idioms and culturally-specific phrases
- Marketing copy in foreign languages
- Asking "why did you translate it this way?"
