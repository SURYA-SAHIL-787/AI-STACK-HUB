## What it does

DeepL translates text between 30+ languages with quality consistently rated above Google Translate (especially for European languages — German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish). Free tier covers most personal use. Pro tier adds document translation, glossary, and higher character limits.

## Best use cases

- Translating any text with quality that "sounds native"
- Reading foreign-language articles
- Drafting emails to non-English contacts
- Students studying foreign languages

## Pricing breakdown

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 5,000 chars per translation, unlimited requests |
| Starter | $8.74/mo | Unlimited translation, document translation, glossary |
| Advanced | $28.74/mo | Faster translation, formal/informal tone |
| Ultimate | $57.49/mo | Highest priority, more glossaries |

## Beginner verdict

If you ever translate anything, install DeepL. Free tier covers personal use. Industry-default for professional translators.
