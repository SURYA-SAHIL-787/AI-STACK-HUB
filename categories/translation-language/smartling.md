## What it does

Smartling is the enterprise translation platform — used by major brands (Lyft, Pinterest, IHG, etc.). AI features include LanguageAI (machine translation tuned for your brand), translation memory, glossaries, and workflow automation. Combines machine translation with human review in optimized workflows.

## Best use cases

- Enterprises with $50k+ annual translation spend
- Brands needing consistent terminology across markets
- Companies with regulated multilingual content
- Marketing teams running global campaigns
