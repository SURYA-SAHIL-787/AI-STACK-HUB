## What it does

DeepL Pro removes the 5,000-character per-request limit, adds document translation (Word/PDF/PowerPoint preserving formatting), glossary support, formality control (formal vs informal), and crucially — your text is not used for training (data privacy).

## Best use cases

- Translators working professionally
- Companies with multilingual documents
- Privacy-sensitive translation (HR, legal)
- Students writing papers in second/third languages

Cheapest professional-grade AI subscription anywhere. If translation is part of your job, $8.74 is a no-brainer.
